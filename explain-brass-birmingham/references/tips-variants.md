# Tips and Variants: Things to Remember, Beginner Tips, Introductory Game

Page references: **EN** = English rulebook, **DE** = German Spielregel (same layout, same printed page numbers).

---

## Things to Remember
**Rulebook:** EN p. 12 · DE S. 12

- Discard **1 card for every action**, including **passing**.
- You may perform the **same action twice** in a turn (e.g. Network places only 1 canal Link, but you may Network again for a second one).
- **Canal Era:** max **1 of your Industry tiles per location**.
- You may **Overbuild your own** Industry tiles at any time.
- You may Overbuild **opponents' Coal Mines / Iron Works** only when **no coal / iron cubes** are left on any Industry tile **or in the Market**.
- You must be **connected to a Merchant (double-arrow) icon** to **sell** cotton, manufactured goods or pottery, and to buy **coal (but not iron)** from the Market.
- No connection needed to use **your own** Breweries' beer; you **must** be connected to **other players'** Breweries, or to **Merchant beer** (only available when selling).
- A location must be **part of your network** to build there, unless you use **that Location card** or a **Wild Location card**. (DE only names "the matching Location card".)
- Unlike the other level 1 Industry tiles, the **level 1 Pottery may be built in the Rail Era**.

## Beginner Tips
**Rulebook:** EN p. 12 · DE S. 12

- **Don't be afraid of loans.** Final income is not worth VPs (it's only a tiebreaker); experienced players often win with low income.
- **Level 2+ tiles stay** at the end of the Canal Era and can **score twice** if built early: a flipped **level 3 Cotton Mill** would score **18 VPs** over both eras. "The Develop action is your friend."
- **Careful developing Breweries:** beer is needed to Sell; having no beer of your own can be devastating.
- Tiles with the rail-era restriction icon can't be built in the Rail Era. Try to **build or develop at least 1 Coal Mine and 1 Iron Works in the Canal Era**, so you don't need a Develop action in the Rail Era to clear them.
- Expanding at the **start of the Rail Era** is hard for beginners: build **rail Links to sources of coal**; if you have no coal access, **build a Coal Mine**.

## Introductory Game
**Rulebook:** EN p. 12 · DE S. 12

A shorter first game: play **only the Canal Era**, with extra scoring so scores better reflect Canal-Era success.

1. Play the whole Canal Era as normal, **including end-of-Canal-Era scoring** (Links and flipped tiles).
2. Then score additionally:
   1. **Money:** 1 VP per **£4** in your player area (**max 15 VPs**).
   2. **Income level:** VPs equal to your income level (**negative = lose VPs**).
   3. **Industry tiles:** score a **second time** all your **level 2 or higher** Industry tiles.

*Interpretation:* "Industry tiles" in step 3 means flipped tiles, since only flipped tiles have scored; the rulebook says "score the points a second time".
