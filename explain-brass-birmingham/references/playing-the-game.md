# Playing the Game: Goal, Rounds, Player Turns, Action List, End of Round

Page references: **EN** = English rulebook, **DE** = German Spielregel (same layout, same printed page numbers).

---

## The Goal
**Rulebook:** EN p. 6 · DE S. 6

- 2 eras: **Canal Era (1770–1830)** and **Rail Era (1830–1870)**.
- Winner: most **VPs** after the end of the Rail Era.
- DE adds: VPs are also gained now and then during play as a Merchant (market) bonus.
- VPs are scored at the end of **each** era for your **Link tiles** and **flipped Industry tiles** (see `end-of-era.md`).

## Rounds
**Rulebook:** EN p. 6 · DE S. 6

- Each era is played in rounds until **both the Draw Deck and all Hands are exhausted**.
- Exactly **8 / 9 / 10 rounds per era** in a **4 / 3 / 2-player** game.
- Each round, players take turns in the order of their **Character tiles on the Turn Order Track**.

## Player Turns
**Rulebook:** EN p. 6 · DE S. 6

- On your turn, perform **2 actions**.
  - **Exception:** in the **first round of the Canal Era**, each player performs only **1 action**.
- For **each** action, **discard 1 card** from your Hand face up onto your Discard Pile.
  - **Exception:** Wild cards go back onto their **Card Draw Areas**, not the Discard Pile.
- After all your actions: **refill your Hand to 8 cards** from the Draw Deck.
- Place **all money spent** during your turn on top of your **Character tile** on the Turn Order Track.
- Once the Draw Deck is exhausted, your Hand shrinks each round until you have no cards left.

## Action List
**Rulebook:** EN p. 6 · DE S. 6 (details p. 9–11, see `actions-build.md` and `actions-other.md`)

For each action choose one of the following; **you may perform the same action twice**.

| # | Action | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Build** | Place one of your Industry tiles on the board, paying its cost and consuming any required coal/iron. |
| 2 | **Network** | Add Link tiles to the board (canal or rail). |
| 3 | **Develop** | Remove Industry tiles from your Player Mat to reach higher-level industries. |
| 4 | **Sell** | Flip your built Cotton Mills, Manufacturers and/or Potteries by selling to a Merchant, consuming beer if necessary. |
| 5 | **Loan** | Take £30 from the Bank, move your Income Marker **3 income levels (not spaces)** back. |
| 6 | **Scout** | Discard **2 additional** cards and take 1 Wild Industry + 1 Wild Location card. **Not allowed if you already have a Wild card in your Hand.** |

- **Passing:** you may pass instead of performing an action, but you **still discard a card** for each action you pass.

## Spending Money
**Rulebook:** EN p. 6 · DE S. 6

- Any money you spend to perform an action goes **on top of your Character tile** on the Turn Order Track (not into the Bank). This determines next round's turn order.

---

## End of Round
**Rulebook:** EN p. 6 · DE S. 6

After all players have taken their turn:

### 1. Determine turn order for the next round
- Rearrange the Character tiles: the player who spent the **least** money this round goes **first**, and so on; the player who spent the **most** goes **last**.
- **Ties:** players who spent the same amount keep their **relative** turn order.
- Then return all money on the Character tiles to the **Bank** (resets spending for the next round).
- Image example (p. 6): players spent £20, £6, £20 → the £6 player moves to first; the two £20 players keep their relative order.

### 2. Take Income
- Take money from the Bank equal to your **income level**.
  - **Exception:** no income is collected at the end of the **final round of the game**.
- Your **income level** is the number on the **coin beside your Income Marker's space** on the Progress Track (example image: Income Marker on space 41 → income level 14).
- **Negative income level:** you must **pay** that amount to the Bank (example image: marker on space 8 → income level –2).

### Shortfall when paying negative income
- If you can't pay, you must raise money by **removing one or more of your Industry tiles** (not Link tiles) from the board; each is worth **half its cost, rounded down**. You keep any excess money.
- If you have no way to pay the shortfall, **lose 1 VP (if possible) for each £1** you are short.
- **Note:** you may choose which of your Industry tiles to remove, but must **stop as soon as** you have enough. You may **never** sell Industry tiles for any reason other than paying a shortfall.

DE S. 6 states it: each tile brings **half of its build costs** (*Hälfte der Baukosten*), rounded down. The EN text only says "half of its cost".
