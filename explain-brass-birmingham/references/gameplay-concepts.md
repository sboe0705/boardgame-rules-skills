# Gameplay Concepts: Flipping, Income, Network, Connections, Consuming Coal / Iron / Beer

Page references: **EN** = English rulebook, **DE** = German Spielregel (same layout, same printed page numbers).

---

## Flipping Industry Tiles
**Rulebook:** EN p. 8 · DE S. 8

- At the end of each era, **flipped** Industry tiles score VPs. Flipped = **black top half** and a **VP icon in the bottom left corner**.
- How tiles flip:
  - **Cotton Mills, Manufacturers, Potteries:** when you perform a **Sell** action with them.
  - **Coal Mines, Iron Works, Breweries:** when the **last resource** (coal / iron / beer) is removed from the tile. This often happens **during an opponent's turn**.
- When a tile flips, its owner advances their Income Marker by the number in the **bottom right corner** (p. 9 step 3, p. 10 Sell step 4). *Follows from the rule:* this also applies when an opponent's consumption empties your tile.

## Increasing Your Income
**Rulebook:** EN p. 8 · DE S. 8

- Whenever your income increases, immediately advance your Income Marker by the number of **spaces** shown (**not income levels**).
- **Exception:** income level can't go **above level 30**.
- (Loans, by contrast, move you back by **income levels**, see `actions-other.md`.)

## Your Network
**Rulebook:** EN p. 8 · DE S. 8

A location is part of **your network** if at least one is true:
- it contains **one or more of your Industry tiles**, or
- it is **adjacent to one or more of your Link tiles**.

(Other players' tiles do not make a location part of your network.)

## Connected Locations
**Rulebook:** EN p. 8 · DE S. 8

- Two locations are **connected** if you can trace a route of **Link tiles owned by any player** from one to the other.
- Connection (any player's Links) matters for coal, opponents' beer and selling; **network** (your own tiles/Links) matters for where you may build and place Links.

## Consuming Coal
**Rulebook:** EN p. 8 · DE S. 8

- Coal is needed to build **rail Links** and certain **Industry tiles**.
- The rail Link or Industry tile must be **connected to a source of coal after it is placed**.
- Coal must be taken in this order:
  1. From the **closest** (fewest Link tiles away) **connected, unflipped Coal Mine** of **any player**. If several are equally close, you choose. If that mine runs out and you need more, take from the next closest. **Free.**
  2. Only if you are **not connected to any unflipped Coal Mine**: buy from the **Coal Market**, cheapest first. This requires a **connection to a Merchant icon** (double-arrow icon on the **Warrington, Shrewsbury, Nottingham, Gloucester and Oxford** Merchants). If the Coal Market is empty you can still buy coal for **£8** each. DE adds: even then a **connection to a Merchant icon is required**.
- Consumed coal goes back to the **General Supply**.
- Historical note: coal was needed in large quantities, so a robust transport network was critical.

## Consuming Iron
**Rulebook:** EN p. 8 · DE S. 8

- Iron is needed for the **Develop** action and to build certain **Industry tiles**.
- **No connection** to an iron source is needed.
- Iron must be taken in this order:
  1. From **any unflipped Iron Works** (any player), **not necessarily the closest**. If you need more than 1, each may come from a different Iron Works. **Free.**
  2. Only if there are **no unflipped Iron Works**: buy from the **Iron Market**, cheapest first. If empty, buy for **£6** each.
- Consumed iron goes back to the General Supply.
- Historical note: needed in low volumes, so it could be moved by horse and wagon.

## Consuming Beer
**Rulebook:** EN p. 8 · DE S. 8

- Beer may be required when **selling** cotton, manufactured goods or pottery, or for the **Network** action in the Rail Era (double rail Link). The amount (if any) is in the **top right corner** of the Industry tile being sold.
- Sources:
  1. **Your own unflipped Breweries** — **no connection needed**.
  2. An **opponent's unflipped Brewery** — must be **connected** to the location where the beer is needed.
  3. The **beer barrel beside the Merchant tile you are selling to** (only during Sell; see `actions-other.md` → Merchant Beer Bonuses).
- When several beers are needed, each may come from a **different source**.
- Consumed beer goes back to the General Supply.
- Historical note: clean water was scarce, so beer was often the safest drink.
