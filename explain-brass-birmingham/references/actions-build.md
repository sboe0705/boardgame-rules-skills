# Build Action (incl. Moving Coal/Iron to the Market, No Tiles on the Board, Farm Breweries, Canal/Rail Era Building, Overbuilding)

Page references: **EN** = English rulebook, **DE** = German Spielregel (same layout, same printed page numbers).

---

## Build Action
**Rulebook:** EN p. 9 · DE S. 9

Place an Industry tile onto a location on the board.

### Step 1: Discard an appropriate card
Unlike other actions (any card), Build needs a **matching card**, discarded face up onto your Discard Pile (**Wild cards** go back to their Card Draw Area):

| Card | Lets you build… |
|---|---|
| **Location card** | **any** Industry tile at the **named location**, **even if it is not part of your network** |
| **Wild Location card** | as any Location card — **but not** the 2 **Farm Breweries** (DE S. 9 does not mention this exception, see Farm Breweries) |
| **Industry card** | the Industry tile matching **an icon in the card's top corner** (DE: top left), in a location that **is part of your network** |
| **Wild Industry card** | as any Industry card |

### Step 2: Place the tile
Take the **lowest level** tile of the chosen industry from your Player Mat and place it (**black-top side face down**) on an **undeveloped space** in the location:
- If possible, on a space showing **only that industry's icon**.
- Otherwise, on a space showing that industry's icon **together with another industry's icon**.
- If no undeveloped space shows the icon, you **can't** build it there (**exception:** Overbuilding, below).
- Image example (Stoke-on-Trent): a Manufacturer must go on the single-icon Manufacturer space before a shared Pottery/Manufacturer space.

### Step 3: Pay
- Cost is printed **to the left of the tile's slot** on your Player Mat.
- Pay the money onto your **Character tile** on the Turn Order Track, and consume any required **iron and coal** (see `gameplay-concepts.md`).
- **Remember:** if coal is required, the build location must be **connected** to a source of coal.

### Step 4: Place resources
| Built tile | Put on it |
|---|---|
| **Coal Mine** | coal cubes = number in the tile's **bottom right corner** |
| **Iron Works** | iron cubes = number in the tile's **bottom right corner** |
| **Brewery** | **1 beer barrel in the Canal Era**, **2 in the Rail Era** |

## Moving Coal and Iron to the Market
**Rulebook:** EN p. 9 · DE S. 9

Applies when you build:
- a **Coal Mine** that is **connected to any Merchant space** (even one **without** a Merchant tile), or
- an **Iron Works**, **whether or not** it is connected.

Then:
1. You **must** immediately move **as many cubes as possible** from the tile to empty spaces in the matching Market, **filling the most expensive spaces first**.
2. For each cube moved, collect the **price on the coin** to the left of its Market space.
3. If the **last cube** leaves your tile, **flip** it and advance your Income Marker by the number in its **bottom right corner**.

- **Note:** cubes may be sold to the Market **only during the action in which the tile is built**, never later.
- Image example: a new level 1 Coal Mine sends both its coal cubes to the most expensive empty Coal Market spaces (coins £4 and £3), collects that money, and flips (its last cube left); an Iron Works likewise sends its cubes to the empty Iron Market spaces, most expensive first.

## Building If You Have No Tiles on the Board
**Rulebook:** EN p. 9 · DE S. 9

If you have **no Industry or Link tiles** on the board, you may (as an action) discard:
- an **Industry card** to build the matching Industry tile in **any location** with an undeveloped space showing that icon;
- **any card** to build a **Link tile on any undeveloped line** (see `actions-other.md` → Network; coal still needed for rail).

## Farm Breweries
**Rulebook:** EN p. 9 · DE S. 9

- 2 **unnamed** locations, each with **1 Brewery space**.
- You may only build there with a **Brewery Industry card** or a **Wild Industry card** (not a Wild Location card, p. 9).
  - **DE differs:** "only with a Brewery card or a Joker card" (*Brauerei- oder einer Jokerkarte*), without naming which Wild card. The EN rule (Wild Industry only, Wild Location explicitly excluded) is the more precise one.
- A Link tile is required to connect **Cannock** to the Farm Brewery to its left.
- A Link tile between **Kidderminster and Worcester** also connects **both** locations to the Farm Brewery to their left. A second Link there is **not required and not allowed**.
- *Follows from the rules:* a Brewery card used this way still requires the Farm Brewery to be part of your network (Industry card rule), unless you have no tiles on the board.

## Build Action (continued)
**Rulebook:** EN p. 10 · DE S. 10

### Canal Era Building
- **Max 1 of your Industry tiles per location**, but you may share a location with other players' tiles. (DE says "each player may build max. 1 Industry tile per location", which implies the same.)
- Tiles with the **canal-era restriction icon** (half-dark circle) to the left of their slot **may not be built** in the Canal Era. DE wording: "may **not (yet)** be built" (*(noch) nicht*), i.e. these tiles become available later.
- Image example (Birmingham): four tiles of four different players.

### Rail Era Building
- You **may build multiple** Industry tiles in each location.
- Tiles with the **rail-era restriction icon** to the left of their slot **may not be built** (DE: "**no longer**", *nicht (mehr)*). To get rid of them (and reach higher levels) you must **Develop** them.
- These are the level 1 tiles (p. 12). **Exception:** the **level 1 Pottery** may be built in the Rail Era (p. 12).

### Overbuilding
Replace a placed Industry tile with a **higher-level tile of the same industry** (you still pay all build costs).

| Replacing… | Allowed | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| **Your own** tile | **any** industry | Cubes (iron/coal) on the replaced tile go back to the General Supply |
| **An opponent's** tile | **only Coal Mine or Iron Works** | **No cubes of that resource anywhere on the board, including its Market** |

- Overbuilt tiles are **removed from the game** (box); they don't score.
- Owners **lose no income or VPs** when overbuilt.
- Image examples: (A) own level I Manufacturer replaced by a level VII Manufacturer; (B) opponent's Iron Works replaced by a level III Iron Works.
- p. 12 adds: you may overbuild your own tiles **at any time**; opponents' Coal Mines/Iron Works only when **no coal/iron cubes** are left on any Industry tile or in the Market.
- EN only says you pay the normal build costs. **DE adds:** "otherwise follow the rules of the Build action", so you also need a valid card for the location and the normal placement rules apply.
