# End of Era Maintenance, Winning the Game

Page references: **EN** = English rulebook, **DE** = German Spielregel (same layout, same printed page numbers).

---

## End of Canal & Rail Era
**Rulebook:** EN p. 7 · DE S. 7

An era ends after the round in which **all players use the final cards in their Hands**. Then:

### 1. Score Canal/Rail Links
- For **each** of your Link tiles: score **1 VP for each Link VP icon** (hexagon with a link symbol) shown in the **two locations the Link connects** ("adjacent locations"). Advance your VP Marker on the Progress Track.
- **Remove** Link tiles from the board as they are scored.
- DE S. 7 states it: the Link icons are shown **on the board and on the Industry tiles**.
- Where the icons come from (from the images): the **top right corner of flipped Industry tiles** (any player's) and the board's **Merchant locations**, which show 2 Link VP icons each. Unflipped tiles show beer requirements there, not Link icons.
- Image example (p. 7): orange Links worth **3, 3, 6 and 7** VPs, each the sum of the icons in its two end locations.

### 2. Score Flipped Industry Tiles
- **After all players have removed their Link tiles**, score the VPs in the **bottom left corner** of each of your **flipped** Industry tiles (black top half).
- **Unflipped Industry tiles do not score.**
- **Note:** above **100 VPs**, record the extra points by starting another loop of the Progress Track.

*Follows from the order:* Link scoring comes first, while all tiles (including level 1 tiles) are still on the board, so they count for Link VPs.

## End of Canal Era (additional steps)
**Rulebook:** EN p. 7 · DE S. 7

After steps 1–2 above, at the end of the **Canal Era** only:

3. **Remove Obsolete Industries:** remove **all level 1 Industry tiles from the board** (not from Player Mats) and return them to the box. **Level 2 or higher tiles stay** on the board (and so can score again in the Rail Era, see `tips-variants.md`).
4. **Reset Merchant Beer:** place 1 beer barrel on each **empty** beer barrel space beside a **non-blank** Merchant tile.
5. **Shuffle Draw Deck:** shuffle all players' Discard Piles together and place them face down on the Draw Deck's Card Draw Area. (DE: "shuffle all played cards".)
   - **Remember:** the **bottom card** of each Discard Pile is face down (the one dealt at setup) and must be turned over before shuffling.

*Not stated in the rulebook:* how players get a new Hand for the Rail Era. The turn rule "refill your Hand back up to 8 cards" (p. 6) is the only refill rule; the rulebook does not describe a separate deal at the start of the Rail Era. Check the latest rulebook / official FAQ (interpretation: players draw back up to 8 from the new Draw Deck).

The rulebook also does not say where scored (removed) Link tiles go. DE S. 1 notes the Link tiles are double-sided (canal / rail).

## Winning the Game
**Rulebook:** EN p. 7 · DE S. 7

After the **Rail Era** scoring:
- Most VPs **wins**.
- Ties: first **highest income** (level), then **most money remaining**.
- Still tied: those players **draw** (DE: they **share the victory**).
- Remember: no income is collected at the end of the final round (p. 6).
