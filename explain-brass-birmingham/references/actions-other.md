# Sell, Loan, Scout, Develop and Network Actions

Page references: **EN** = English rulebook, **DE** = German Spielregel (same layout, same printed page numbers).

All these actions start by **discarding any card** from your Hand face up onto your Discard Pile (**Wild cards** go back to their Card Draw Area instead). Scout discards 3 cards.

---

## Sell Action
**Rulebook:** EN p. 10 · DE S. 10

Flips your built **Cotton Mills, Manufacturers and Potteries**. Requires a **connection to a Merchant tile** and (usually) **beer**.

1. Discard any card.
2. Choose 1 of your **unflipped** Cotton Mill, Manufacturer or Pottery tiles that is **connected to a Merchant tile showing that industry's icon**.
3. Consume the **beer** shown in the tile's **top right corner**. If there is a **beer barrel beside the Merchant tile you sell to**, you may consume that one as part of the Sell and collect its **Merchant beer bonus**.
4. **Flip** the tile and advance your Income Marker by the number in its **bottom right corner**.
5. You may go **back to step 2** and repeat (DE: "repeat steps 2 and 3 as often as you like") for each of your unflipped tiles (also of a **different industry**) — all in one action with one card.

- **Note:** you can't Sell if you can't consume the required beer (see `gameplay-concepts.md` → Consuming Beer).

### Merchant Beer Bonuses
**Rulebook:** EN p. 10 · DE S. 10

Merchant beer may **only** be consumed as part of a **Sell** action. If you consume it, you also get the bonus shown beside that Merchant's location:

| Merchant | Bonus |
|---|---|
| **Gloucester** | **Develop:** remove 1 of the lowest-level tiles of any industry from your Player Mat. The bonus text names no iron cost; whether iron is needed is not stated. **Exception:** not a Pottery tile with a **lightbulb icon**. |
| **Oxford** | **Income:** advance your Income Marker **2 spaces** |
| **Nottingham**, **Shrewsbury** | **VPs:** advance your VP Marker by the number shown (board image: Nottingham 3, Shrewsbury 4) |
| **Warrington** | **Money:** £5 from the Bank |

## Loan Action
**Rulebook:** EN p. 11 · DE S. 11

Loans are **never repaid**; they lower your income level instead.
1. Discard any card.
2. Take **£30** from the Bank and move your Income Marker **3 income levels (not spaces)** back. Place it on the **highest space within** that lower income level.
- **Exception:** no loan if it would take your income level **below –10**.

## Scout Action
**Rulebook:** EN p. 11 · DE S. 11

Gets you Wild cards for more flexibility where you build.
1. Discard **any 3 cards** from your Hand face up onto your Discard Pile (the action list on p. 6 says "discard 2 **additional** cards", i.e. 3 in total).
2. Take **1 Wild Location** and **1 Wild Industry** card.
- **Not allowed** if you already have a Wild card in your Hand.
- Wild Industry = any Industry card; Wild Location = any Location card. Discarded Wild cards return to their Card Draw Area, never to your Discard Pile.

## Develop Action
**Rulebook:** EN p. 11 · DE S. 11

Removes Industry tiles from your Player Mat, giving access to higher levels without building all lower levels.
1. Discard any card.
2. Remove **1 or 2** Industry tiles from your Player Mat to the box. Each is removed separately, they need not be the same industry, but each must be the **lowest-level tile of its industry** at the moment it's removed (*follows from this:* two tiles of the same industry may be removed one after the other).
3. **Consume 1 iron per tile removed** (see Consuming Iron — no connection needed).

### Potteries and the Lightbulb Icon
- Pottery tiles with a **lightbulb icon** **may not be developed**. They can only leave your Player Mat by being **built**, and must be removed before you can reach higher-level Pottery tiles.

## Network Action
**Rulebook:** EN p. 11 · DE S. 11

Place Link tiles to expand your network.
1. Discard any card.
2. Place a Link tile on an **undeveloped line**. It must be **adjacent to a location in your network**.
   - **Exception:** with **no Industry or Link tiles** on the board you may use **any** undeveloped line (still consume coal for a rail Link; DE says instead "for a rail Link you must pay the costs").

### Network during the Canal Era
- **No rail Links.**
- Canal Links only, on undeveloped **canal lines**.
- **Max 1 canal Link** per Network action, for **£3**. (To build two, take the Network action twice, p. 12.)

### Network during the Rail Era
- **No canal Links.**
- Rail Links only, on undeveloped **railroad lines**.
- **1 rail Link for £5**, or **max 2 rail Links for £15** if you also consume **1 beer** from a **Brewery** (not Merchant beer).
  - If the beer comes from **another player's Brewery**, it must be **connected to the second rail Link** (after it's placed).
- **1 coal per rail Link.** Each rail Link is placed separately and must be **connected to a coal source after it is placed**.
