# Game Setup (Board Setup, Location Banners, Player Area Setup)

Page references: **EN** = English rulebook, **DE** = German Spielregel (same layout, same printed page numbers; see `components.md` for the PDF page mapping).

---

## Board Setup (10 steps)
**Rulebook:** EN p. 4 · DE S. 4

1. Place the board on either the **day or night side**.
2. **Fewer than 4 players:** remove all **cards and Merchant tiles** that show a player count **greater** than your number of players; return them to the box.
   - Player counts (2, 3 or 4 person icons) are in the **bottom right corner of the card fronts** and **along the top of the Merchant tiles**.
3. Separate the **Wild Location** and **Wild Industry** cards and place them **face up** on 2 of the Card Draw Areas.
4. Shuffle the remaining cards and place them **face down** on the remaining Card Draw Area: this is the **Draw Deck**.
5. Shuffle the remaining Merchant tiles and place **1 face up** on each **Merchant space marked with your player count** (near the board edges).
   - **Result:** 2 players → no Merchant tiles in **Warrington** and **Nottingham**. 3 players → no Merchant tiles in **Nottingham**.
6. Place **1 beer barrel** on each beer barrel space beside a **non-blank** Merchant tile.
7. Place 1 **black cube** on each space of the **Coal Market**, leaving **1 of the £1 spaces** open.
8. Place 1 **orange cube** on each space of the **Iron Market**, leaving **both £1 spaces** open.
9. Remaining cubes and beer barrels go beside the board: the **General Supply**.
10. Money beside the board: the **Bank**.

*Derived from the board image (p. 5, 8):* Coal Market has 14 spaces (2 each at £1–£7) → **13 coal** at start; Iron Market has 10 spaces (2 each at £1–£5) → **8 iron** at start.
DE S. 4 words steps 6–8 more concretely: beer goes beside each Merchant tile **with industry icon(s)** (= non-blank); coal on the spaces **£2–£7 plus one £1 space**; iron on the spaces **£2–£5**.

## Location Banners
**Rulebook:** EN p. 4 · DE S. 4

The banner colour of a location shows which **Location cards** are in the Draw Deck:

| Players | Location cards in the deck |
|---|---|
| 2 | **blue** and **teal** Location cards are **not** in the deck |
| 3 | **teal** Location cards are **not** in the deck |
| 4 | all Location cards |

*Interpretation (not stated outright):* these are the cards removed in step 2 for showing a higher player count. The locations themselves stay on the board; you just can't build there with that location's card (you can still build there through your network with an Industry card, or with a Wild Location card).

## Player Area Setup
**Rulebook:** EN p. 5 · DE S. 5

Each player:
1. Takes a **Player Mat**.
2. Takes **£17** from the Bank.
3. Chooses a coloured **Character tile**.
4. Places the matching coloured **Link tiles** in their player area.
5. Stacks the matching coloured **Industry tiles** (Cotton Mills, Coal Mines, Iron Works, Manufacturers, Potteries, Breweries) on their slots on the Player Mat, **black-top-half side face down**.
6. Places the **VP Marker** on space **"0"** of the Progress Track.
7. Places the **Income Marker** on space **"10"** of the Progress Track (this is income level £0 on the track, as the board image shows).
8. Draws **8 cards** from the Draw Deck as their **Hand** (kept hidden).
9. Draws **1 more card** and places it **face down** in their player area: this starts the **Discard Pile**.

Then shuffle all players' Character tiles and place them in **random order** on the **Turn Order Track**.
