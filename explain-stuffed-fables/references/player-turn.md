# Player Turn (Stuffy Turn)

Page references: **EN** = English rulebook, **DE** = German Spielanleitung (same page layout).

## Turn Overview
**Rulebook:** EN p. 8 · DE S. 8

On a new page the bookkeeper goes first; then play goes clockwise (to the left). On your turn, do these steps **in order**:

1. **Draw Dice** (5 from the bag)
2. **Find Stuffing** (white dice)
3. **Place Threat** (black dice)
4. **Perform Actions** (attribute dice): Move · Reserve · Encourage · Skill Test · Group Task · Attack · Search
5. **Discard Dice**
6. **Check Threat**

---

## 1. Draw Dice
**Rulebook:** EN p. 8 · DE S. 8

- Draw **5 dice** from the dice bag and put them in front of you.
- If there are fewer than 5 dice in the bag: draw all remaining dice, then move **all dice from the discard pool back into the bag**, then finish your draw.

## 2. Find Stuffing (white dice)
**Rulebook:** EN p. 8 · DE S. 8

- Roll your **white** dice to find stuffing. Each die may be rolled **separately**, or **several together as one roll** (sum them, to get a higher result).
- After each roll: if the result is **equal to or higher than your stuffy's current number of stuffing**, gain **1 stuffing**, and discard the die (or dice) used to the discard pool.
- The rulebook only says what happens on a success (gain 1 stuffing, discard the die). It does not say explicitly what happens to a white die that failed; any white dice still in front of you are discarded at step 5 at the latest. *(If asked, call this an interpretation.)*
- **No limit** to how much stuffing a stuffy can have.
- Follows from the rule (not stated outright): a collapsed stuffy (0 stuffing) succeeds with any roll, so it always regains 1 stuffing if it drew a white die (see damage-status-end.md → Collapsed).

## 3. Place Threat (black dice)
**Rulebook:** EN p. 8 · DE S. 8

- Place **all** your black dice on the **threat track**, starting at the top. (They are not rolled now; they're rolled later when minions activate.)

## 4. Perform Actions
**Rulebook:** EN p. 8 · DE S. 8

- Your remaining colored (attribute) dice can be used for actions. To perform an action you **spend** a die of the right color: **roll it, resolve its effect, then discard it**.
- As with white dice, dice can be rolled **one at a time** or **several of the same color (and/or purple) together as one roll** (results added).
- You may perform **as many actions as you like, as often as you like**, in any order, as long as you have suitable dice.

### What each die color can do
**Rulebook:** EN p. 8 · DE S. 8

| Die | Actions |
|---|---|
| **Any color** attribute die | **Move** (across dotted lines, or across solid lines of the die's color) · **Reserve** (a die) · **Encourage** (a stuffy) · **Skill test** of the matching color · **Contribute to a group task** of the matching color |
| **Red** | + **Melee attack** |
| **Green** | + **Ranged attack** |
| **Yellow** | + **Search** |
| **Blue** | no unique action (only the general ones; important for defending/blue tests via items & abilities) |
| **Purple** (DE: **pink**) | can **substitute for any color** of attribute die |

---

### Move
**Rulebook:** EN p. 9 · DE S. 9

- Spend dice of **any color**: move your stuffy **up to** a number of spaces equal to the roll result.
- Move **orthogonally and/or diagonally**, only on the grid; never onto bracketed spaces.
- You may move **through** a space with another stuffy but **not end** your movement there.
- You **cannot move through a space with a minion**.
- You may only cross a **solid colored line** if you rolled that color and/or purple dice for the move. (So if you roll a red + blue together, you may cross red and blue solid lines in that move.)
- **Solid white lines can never be crossed.**
- Moving onto/over a Lost token, point of interest or vendor stops movement (see setup.md).

### Reserve
**Rulebook:** EN p. 9 · DE S. 9

- Instead of spending a die, **reserve** it: place it (unrolled) in the **die storage slot** on your character card.
- A reserved die can be spent later to **defend against damage**, or spent on one of your **future turns** (as an extra die for actions).
- You **cannot reserve** if there is already a die on your card.
- Stored dice stay on the card when you discard dice in step 5 (Storybook page changes also say "return all dice, except those stored on cards, to the dice bag").

### Encourage
**Rulebook:** EN p. 9 · DE S. 9

Choose **another player's stuffy** and do **one** of:
- **Store 1 of your dice** on that stuffy's card (like reserving). If it already has a stored die, the previously stored die is discarded.
- **Discard any color die** and **give that stuffy 1 of your stuffing**. (This is how a collapsed stuffy can be revived by others.)

### Skill Test
**Rulebook:** EN p. 9 · DE S. 9

- Skill tests have a **color** and a **target difficulty**, written e.g. "Make a yellow/perception skill test, difficulty 4. PASS: … FAIL: …" (DE: "Führe eine gelbe Wahrnehmungsprobe (4) durch. ERFOLG: … FEHLSCHLAG: …").
- Colors and their names: **red/strength** (DE rote Stärkeprobe), **green/agility** (grüne Beweglichkeitsprobe), **yellow/perception** (gelbe Wahrnehmungsprobe), **blue** (DE blaue Widerstandsprobe).
- To perform: spend **any number** of dice of that color (or purple) as one roll. **Result ≥ target → PASS** effect; otherwise **FAIL** effect.
- If you are **required** to make a skill test but have no suitable dice, you **automatically fail**.
- Where skill tests come from:
  - **Storybook special rules**
  - **Status cards** (many must be passed to remove the status)
  - **Environment cards** (to interact with the environment)
  - **Lost cards**

### Group Task
**Rulebook:** EN p. 9 · DE S. 9

- Like a skill test, but **multiple players contribute on their turns**: roll dice of the right color (or purple) and choose to place them on the **group task track** of the sideboard.
- When the **total on the track ≥ target difficulty**: discard those dice and resolve the task's **PASS** effect.
- Group tasks **cannot fail**; contributions stay on the track across turns until complete.
- Stuffies can contribute **from anywhere on the map**, unless the Storybook says otherwise (e.g. "only stuffies adjacent to the door can contribute").
- Example (EN p. 9): hut door needs a red/strength group task, difficulty 13. Lumpy rolls a 5 on a red die and adds it to the track; not enough yet – with another red or a purple die he could keep adding.

### Melee Attack (red)
**Rulebook:** EN p. 10 · DE S. 10

- Requires an equipped **melee weapon**.
- Target a minion in an **adjacent** space with **no colored lines between** you (a line fully separating the two spaces blocks the attack; lines that don't fully separate the spaces don't block).
- Roll **any number of red dice** (and/or purple). **Result ≥ minion's defense → minion defeated.**
- Defeated minion: remove its figure from the map, discard its card. The stuffy that defeated it gains **1 button**.
- If the minion was a **leader or boss**, the stuffy also gains a **Courageous** status card (DE *Mutig*).
- **Note:** when a minion is defeated, its black threat dice **stay on the threat track**.
- Example: Lumpy cannot attack crawly A with his Meat Mallet because a colored line is between them; Theadora can attack crawly B with her Letter Opener because the lines don't fully block it.

### Ranged Attack (green)
**Rulebook:** EN p. 10 · DE S. 10

- Works like a melee attack, except:
  - requires an equipped **ranged weapon**,
  - uses **green dice** (and/or purple),
  - may target a minion up to the weapon's **range** in spaces away (range counted like movement, DE S. 10/14),
  - may target **across solid colored lines** (except **solid white lines**).
- Example: Flops can attack crawly A on the bed with her Archery Set (ranged, range 6).

### Attacking a Boss
**Rulebook:** EN p. 10 · DE S. 10 (see also EN p. 7)

- When a boss would be defeated by an attack, instead **remove 1 dark stuffing** from its card. When no dark stuffing remains, it is defeated and removed from play.

### Search (yellow)
**Rulebook:** EN p. 10 · DE S. 10

- Most maps have a **search icon with a target number** in the upper-right corner.
- Spend **any number of yellow dice** (and/or purple). **Result ≥ search number → draw an item card.**
- When drawing an item (not a party item): **equip** it to your stuffy **or trade** it to another player, who must equip it immediately.
- Drawing a **party item**: place it within reach of all players.
- Searching can be repeated as long as you have yellow/purple dice.

### Equipment Slots
**Rulebook:** EN p. 10 · DE S. 10

- Each stuffy has 4 slots: **Head, Body, Paws, Accessory**.
- A stuffy cannot have 2 items with the **same equipment slot icon**. To equip a new item in an occupied slot, discard the old one.
- Example: Theadora has the Letter Opener (paws). She finds Scissors (paws). She can't have both; to equip the Scissors she must discard the Letter Opener.

### Party Items
**Rulebook:** EN p. 10 · DE S. 10

- Party items (DE: *Gruppengegenstände*, keyword "Gruppe") aren't equipped; they're placed within reach of everyone and count as **equipped to all stuffies at the same time**.
- **No limit** to the number of party items in play.
- Example: **Tricycle** – place face up on the sleep deck; the next time you would reveal a sleep card, discard the Tricycle instead.

### Bonuses and Purple Dice
**Rulebook:** EN p. 11 · DE S. 11

- Cards/effects give **bonuses** to die rolls, usually only for **dice of a certain color**.
- Bonuses are applied **after** effects that change a die result (e.g. re-rolls).
- **Purple** dice may be used **in place of any other attribute die**, but a purple die does **not become** that color: purple dice **don't benefit from bonuses** except those specifically for purple dice.
- Example: Sarah melee attacks with 2 red + 1 purple; her Meat Mallet gives red dice +1. She rolls red 3, red 1, purple 4 → **3+1 + 1+1 + 4 = 10**.
- **You may** use a purple die in place of: a red die for a melee attack; a green die to move across a green line; a yellow die to search; a blue die for a blue skill test.
- **You may NOT:** use Lumpy's ability to re-roll purple dice (it only re-rolls blue); add +1 to purple dice when attacking with the Rubber Bands (they only add +1 to green dice).

---

## 5. Discard Dice
**Rulebook:** EN p. 11 · DE S. 11

- Discard **all** your remaining (unused) dice to the discard pool. (Dice stored on character cards stay.)

## 6. Check Threat
**Rulebook:** EN p. 11 · DE S. 11

**If minions are in play:** if the number of dice on the threat track **≥ number of minions in play**, the **minions take a turn** (see minion-turn.md).

**If no minions are in play:** if the number of dice on the threat track **≥ number of stuffies in play**, a **surge** happens. Resolve in order:
1. Resolve the **surge effect** on the current Storybook page (usually "First Surge: …" and "Additional Surges: …", e.g. "First Surge: go to section 7-3. Additional Surges: reveal a sleep card." DE: *Erste Bedrohung / Jede weitere Bedrohung*).
2. If the surge brought minions into play, they **immediately take a minion turn**.
3. **Discard all dice from the threat track.**
4. **Refresh the dice bag** (return all dice in the discard pool to the bag).

After Check Threat (and any minion turn), the **next player to the left** takes their turn.

Notes:
- If the threshold isn't met, threat dice simply stay on the track and accumulate.
- The threat track has 5 spaces.
