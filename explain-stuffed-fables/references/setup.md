# Game Setup, Page Setup, Reading a Map, Leaving a Page, Encounters

Page references: **EN** = English rulebook, **DE** = German Spielanleitung (same page layout).

---

## Game Setup (10 steps)
**Rulebook:** EN p. 5 · DE S. 5

1. **Choose stuffies.** Each player picks a stuffy character card and its figure and puts **5 stuffing tokens** (light side up) on the card. (Some stories don't allow every stuffy; the story start says so.)
   - **2-player game:** players *may* control 2 stuffies each. For rules purposes, a player with two stuffies counts as **2 players with 2 separate turns**.
2. **Fill the dice bag:** all attribute dice (purple, blue, green, red, yellow) + **white stuffing dice = number of stuffies + 1** + **black threat dice = number of stuffies + 1**.
   - Example (DE): 3 stuffies → 4 white + 4 black.
3. Shuffle the **item cards** → item deck.
4. Shuffle the **Lost cards** → Lost deck.
5. Make a **face-up pile of boss minion cards**, then shuffle the remaining minion cards → minion deck. (In your first game there are no boss cards yet; they're added through the stories.)
6. **Build the sleep deck:** shuffle the **Waking** card with **2 random sleep cards** and place them face down. Shuffle the remaining sleep cards and place them face down **on top** of that. (So Waking is always among the bottom 3 cards.)
7. Put status cards, buttons, stuffing tokens, heart tokens, etc. within easy reach.
8. Put the **Storybook** in the middle of the table and the **sideboard** to its right.
9. Choose the first **bookkeeper** and give them the **bookmark token**. The bookkeeper takes the first turn; play goes to the **left** (clockwise).
10. Choose a story, open the Storybook to its start page and read the first section. (First game: start with story 1, **The Big Girl Bed** / DE *Ein Bett für große Mädchen*.) DE adds: there's an overview of the stories on the last page of the Storybook; leave room for a **discard pool** (DE *Ablagebereich*) for spent dice.

Story start: gather that story's **discovery cards** without looking at them and place them face down near the play area (see components.md → Discovery Deck).

### The bookkeeper
- The bookkeeper reads the Storybook aloud, decides ties (e.g. where minions are placed, which equally-close stuffy a minion targets or moves toward) and always takes the **first turn on a new page** (DE S. 8: "Auf einer neuen Seite ist der Buchwächter immer zuerst am Zug").
- The Storybook itself tells you when the bookkeeper role passes to the next player (typically at a page change: "The bookkeeper passes the bookmark to the player on her left").

## Page Setup
**Rulebook:** EN p. 6 · DE S. 6

Each page of the Storybook gives its own setup instructions (SETUP box). The general rules for reading a map and generating encounters are below.

## Reading a Map
**Rulebook:** EN p. 6 · DE S. 6

1. **Search difficulty** (search icon + number in the upper-right corner of the map): target number for a successful search.
2. **Colored lines:** to move across a **solid** colored line, you must use a die of the matching color (or purple). Dotted lines can be crossed with any die. **Solid white lines can never be crossed.**
3. **The Lost** (speech-bubble icon): see below.
4. **Points of interest** (eye icon with a number): see below.
5. **Stuffy start space:** stuffies enter play **on or adjacent** (orthogonally or diagonally) to this space.
6. **Minion start space** (icon of a circle with a claw/arrow): minions entering via an encounter are placed on or adjacent to this space.
7. **Vendors** (button icon): see below.
8. **Bracketed spaces** (only the corners are drawn): used **only for counting range**. Figures cannot move onto them unless the Storybook says so. (DE: also cannot move *through* them.)

### The Lost
**Rulebook:** EN p. 6 · DE S. 6

- Spaces with the Lost (speech-bubble) icon get a **Lost token** at some point during the page.
- If a stuffy **starts, ends or moves over** a space with a Lost token: **end that stuffy's movement, pause its turn, discard the token**.
- The **player to the left of the active player** draws a **Lost card** and reads it aloud.
- Cards usually offer choices to help needy Lost toys; often rewarded, sometimes just a "thank you".
- Stuffies can **always choose to walk away with no effect**, unless the card specifies an effect.
- **Do NOT read the result until the choice has been made.** (DE: read only the choice first, then only the chosen result.)
- After the card resolves, resume play (the paused turn continues) unless directed otherwise.

### Points of Interest
**Rulebook:** EN p. 6 · DE S. 6

- Spaces with the eye symbol (with a number) can be **explored**.
- If a stuffy **starts, ends or moves over** an explorable point of interest on its turn: **end its movement, pause its turn, read the matching numbered entry** in the Storybook page sidebar. Then resume play unless directed otherwise.
- Most points of interest can be explored **only once**. Those whose symbol includes a **"repeat" (circular arrows) icon** can be explored **multiple times**.

### Vendors
**Rulebook:** EN p. 6 · DE S. 6

- Vendor spaces (button symbol) let you trade with a local collector.
- The **first time a stuffy enters each vendor space**: end its movement, pause its turn, **reveal 4 item cards** from the item deck.
- Stuffies **on or adjacent** to the vendor space may trade for one or more of those cards, paying **1 button or discarding 1 item card per card received**.
- Remaining cards stay next to the map for further trading until play moves to a **new page**; then shuffle them back into the item deck.
- DE wording: every time a stuffy is on a vendor space its movement ends and its turn pauses; the 4 cards are only revealed on the **first** visit.

## Leaving a Page
**Rulebook:** EN p. 7 · DE S. 7

- When the Storybook directs you to leave a page, **finish the current player's turn first** (unless stated otherwise).
- Then remove all remaining **minions and tokens** on that page from play (DE: they're out of the game) and move all stuffy figures onto their character cards. Then turn to the new page.

## Generating an Encounter
**Rulebook:** EN p. 7 · DE S. 7

Minions enter play only when the Storybook says to **generate an encounter**. Two types:

### Random Encounter
- Shuffle the **minion deck**. Deal cards **equal to the number of stuffies in play**, face up, in a **column** next to the sideboard (the **minion column**; order = activation order, top to bottom).
- Place each matching figure **on or adjacent to** any minion start space(s). The **bookkeeper chooses** which space each figure goes to.

### Named Encounter
- The Storybook says exactly which minions and how many.
- Search the **minion deck and the face-up boss minion cards** for them.
- **Note:** if a **leader** of that minion type could be chosen, it **must** be chosen.
- **Shuffle** the collected minion cards, deal them face up in a column next to the sideboard, then place figures on/adjacent to minion start spaces (bookkeeper chooses).

### Encountering a Boss
- When a **boss** enters play, place **stuffing tokens dark side up** on its card **equal to the number of stuffies in play**.
- When a boss would be defeated, instead **remove 1 dark stuffing** from its card. When it has **no dark stuffing** left, it is defeated and removed from play. (Same rule: EN p. 10 "Attacking a Boss".)
