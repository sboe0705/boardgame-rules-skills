# Damage, Defending, Status Cards, Collapsed, Sleep Cards, Buttons & Hearts, End of Game

Page references: **EN** = English rulebook, **DE** = German Spielanleitung (same page layout). In the German rulebook these sections are grouped under "Schaden & Zustände" (DE S. 13).

---

## Defending (against any damage)
**Rulebook:** EN p. 13 (attack procedure EN p. 12) · DE S. 12–13

- Sometimes the Storybook or a card says a stuffy **receives X damage**. Stuffies defend against it **the same way as against a minion attack**:
  - roll the **die stored on the stuffy's card**, block damage up to the result (plus applicable defending bonuses),
  - lose 1 stuffing per unblocked damage,
  - if **all** damage was blocked, the die returns to the card; otherwise it is discarded.
- Only a die **stored on the card** (reserved, or given via Encourage) can be used to defend. A stuffy without a stored die cannot defend.
- Related abilities/items: Flops's *Dodge* (double a green defending die), Lumpy's *Protect* / *Reinforced Stitching*, Plastic Helmet (discard to ignore all damage from one source), Play Clay Lid (+1 blue, +2 vs ranged), Pleather Vest (+1 purple).

## Status Cards
**Rulebook:** EN p. 13 (card anatomy EN p. 4) · DE S. 13 (S. 4)

- Stuffies can be affected by conditions such as **scared, angry, torn**, **worried**, **courageous**, **trapped**, **soggy** … (DE examples: *verängstigt, wütend, mutig, besorgt*).
- When a stuffy **"becomes"** something (DE: "ist/wird …"), place the matching status card on it. E.g. "becomes worried" → put a *Worried* card on it.
- A stuffy **cannot have two status cards of the same type**.
- Many status cards have a **skill test** that removes them (see the card). Theadora's *Persevere* removes status cards.
- A stuffy that defeats a **leader or boss** gains a **Courageous** status card (EN p. 10).
- When a stuffy **collapses**, it discards all its status cards.

## Collapsed
**Rulebook:** EN p. 13 · DE S. 13

- A stuffy that **loses all its stuffing** becomes **collapsed**:
  - tip its figure **on its side**,
  - **discard all its status cards, buttons and hearts**,
  - **reveal a sleep card**.
- A collapsed stuffy **cannot take actions, cannot have a die stored on it, cannot use item cards**.
- It **still takes its turn**: draw dice, Find Stuffing, etc. If it is **still collapsed after the Find Stuffing step**, it can't take actions that turn.
- **Minions ignore collapsed stuffies** (they don't move toward or target them).
- A stuffy **returns** from being collapsed as soon as it **gains 1 or more stuffing**, either:
  - by **finding stuffing** on its own turn with a white stuffing die, or
  - when another stuffy uses **Encourage** to give it 1 stuffing.

## Sleep Cards / Sleep Deck
**Rulebook:** EN p. 13 (setup EN p. 5, cards EN p. 4) · DE S. 13 (S. 5, S. 4)

- Reveal a card from the sleep deck **each time a stuffy collapses** or when the **Storybook directs** you to.
- **Sleeping** cards (DE *Ruhiger Schlaf*): **no effect**.
- **Restless** cards (DE *Unruhiger Schlaf*): may have an effect **if one is described in the sidebar of the current Storybook page**.
- **Waking** card (DE *Aufgewacht!*): once revealed, players **no longer reveal sleep cards** when told to (DE: until the end of the story). If the stuffies **win**, the **Waking ending** is read **instead of the Asleep ending** (DE: *Aufgewacht!-Ende* instead of *Ruhiger-Schlaf-Ende*).
- The Waking card is shuffled into the bottom 3 cards during setup, so it comes up late.
- The **Tricycle** party item can be discarded instead of revealing a sleep card once.
- Revealing Waking does **not** make you lose; it changes which ending you get.

## Buttons
**Rulebook:** EN p. 13 · DE S. 13

- When a stuffy **defeats a minion**, it gains **1 button**.
- The game tells you when buttons can be spent (e.g. at **vendors**: 1 button per item, EN p. 6). Spent buttons return to the supply.
- Lost when the stuffy collapses.

## Hearts
**Rulebook:** EN p. 13 · DE S. 13

- The Storybook grants hearts for story-related deeds ("Gain 1 heart").
- **Spend 1 heart** to use one **earned ability** on your stuffy's card.
- The **active ability** can be used **without** spending a heart.
- Both kinds of ability can be used **at any time**, unless the stuffy's card says otherwise (many abilities name their own timing, e.g. "after rolling dice", "before attacking", "once per turn").
- Spent hearts return to the supply. Hearts are lost when the stuffy collapses.

## End of Game
**Rulebook:** EN p. 13 · DE S. 13

- **Lose:** if **all stuffies are collapsed at the same time**.
- **Win:** if the stuffies complete their quest as directed by the Storybook (DE: the Storybook tells you when you've won and which ending to read: Waking or Asleep, depending on whether the Waking sleep card came up).
