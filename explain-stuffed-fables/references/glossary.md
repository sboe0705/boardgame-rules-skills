# Glossary

**Rulebook:** EN p. 14 · DE S. 14 (Glossar). Each entry: English term (German term), then the definition. Where the German glossary adds something, it's marked *DE*.

- **Adjacent** (*benachbart*): the **8 spaces** around a space (orthogonal and diagonal).
- **Attribute Dice** (*Aktionswürfel*): dice for actions on your turn: **red, green, yellow, blue, purple** (DE: pink).
- **Button** (*Knopf*): in-game currency. A stuffy gets one each time it defeats a minion; the game says when buttons can be spent.
- **Collapsed** (*zusammengefallen*): a stuffy that has lost all its stuffing. *DE:* its figure is laid on its side.
- **Defend** (*verteidigen*): roll a die stored on a character card to block 1 or more damage.
- **Discard** (*ablegen / abwerfen*): discarding a **die** moves it to the **discard pool** (*Ablagebereich*); discarding a **card** puts it on the discard pile of the deck it came from.
- **Encounter** (*Begegnung*): when minions are brought into play.
- **Encourage** (*unterstützen*): action to give 1 of your stuffy's stuffing to another stuffy, or to store 1 of your dice on another player's stuffy card.
- **Equipment Slot** (*Ausrüstungsplatz*): Head, Body, Paws, Accessory (*Kopf, Körper, Pfoten, Zubehör*); each slot holds only 1 card. *DE:* a stuffy can't have 2 items showing the same equipment icon.
- **Figure** (*Figur*): the miniatures for stuffies and minions in play.
- **Group Task** (*Gruppenaufgabe*): a task that several stuffies can add attribute dice to on each of their turns.
- **Heart** (*Herz*): granted for story tasks; spend to use 1 of your stuffy's earned abilities (*Herz-Fähigkeiten*).
- **Item** (*Gegenstand*): a card type gained by searching.
- **Map** (*Spielplan*): a Storybook page that figures are placed on and moved around.
- **Minion** (*Monster*): the enemies (figure + card). 3 types: **Normal, Leader, Boss** (*Normal, Anführer, Obermonster*).
- **Minion Column** (*Monsterreihe*): column of minion cards made when generating an encounter; sets activation order, top to bottom.
- **Minion Turn** (*Monster-Spielzug*): a turn in which each minion activates once.
- **Party Item** (*Gruppengegenstand*): an item that, once found, is placed within reach of all players and is usable by everyone. *DE:* identified by the keyword "Gruppe".
- **Range** (*Reichweite*): how many spaces away an attack can reach. *DE:* spaces are counted the same way as for movement.
- **Skill Test** (*Würfelprobe*): a test with a target number; roll dice of a specific color; result must meet or exceed the target to pass. *DE:* written with the difficulty in brackets, e.g. "gelbe Wahrnehmungsprobe (4)"; outcome ERFOLG / FEHLSCHLAG.
- **Sleep Deck** (*Schlafstapel*): reveal a card each time a stuffy collapses and whenever the Storybook says so. Once **Waking** is revealed, no more sleep cards are revealed, and the **Waking ending** triggers (instead of the **Asleep** ending) if the stuffies win.
- **Space** (*Feld*): one square of the grid on a map.
- **Status Card** (*Zustandskarte*): effects attached to stuffies when the game says so.
- **Stuffing Dice** (*Wattewürfel*): **white** dice that let a stuffy regain stuffing in the Find Stuffing step.
- **Stuffy** (*Stoffi*): the stuffed-animal characters the players play.
- **Surge** (*Bedrohung / Bedrohungseffekt*): when **no minions** are in play and dice on the threat track **≥ number of stuffies**, a surge happens (surge icon). Each page has its own surge effect(s).
- **Threat Dice** (*Bedrohungswürfel*): **black** dice representing growing danger; used to determine minion actions on a minion turn.
- **Threat Track** (*Bedrohungsleiste*): the track on the sideboard where black dice are placed when drawn. *DE:* the right track of the sideboard.
- *DE only:* **Refresh the dice bag** (*Würfelbeutel auffüllen*): return all dice from the discard pool into the dice bag.
