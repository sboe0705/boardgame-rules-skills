# Components, Card Anatomy & Overview

Page references: **EN** = English rulebook (*Stuffed Fables*, Z-Man/Plaid Hat); **DE** = German Spielanleitung (*Der Herr der Träume*, Asmodee). Both rulebooks have 16 pages with the same page layout.

---

## Object of the Game
**Rulebook:** EN p. 2 · DE S. 2

- Cooperative adventure game for **2–4 players**.
- Players are a young girl's **stuffed animals ("stuffies")** who protect her from the **minions** lurking under her bed (sent by **Crepitus, the Nightmare King**).
- Using teamwork, courage, compassion and a little luck, the stuffies play through a series of interactive stories in the **Storybook** (DE adds: 7 stories; the whole adventure is played inside the Storybook, which holds the stories, the maps of the places you visit and extra rules for special situations).
- There is no game board apart from the Storybook: each spread is a **map** (left/main area) with story text and special rules.

## Storybook
**Rulebook:** EN p. 3 · DE S. 3

- Each story starts on a story start page; the **bookkeeper** reads sections aloud (sections are numbered *page-section*, e.g. "3-1", "25-2").
- Story pages contain: the map, numbered text sections, the page's **surge** effect (DE: *Bedrohungseffekt*), SETUP and SPECIAL RULES boxes, explorable point-of-interest entries, and (sometimes) a *Restless* sleep-card effect in the sidebar.
- Some stories restrict which stuffies can be chosen (e.g. in story 1, *The Big Girl Bed*, Piggle and Lionel are not available because they haven't been met yet). (DE S. 5 also mentions this: a note at the start of the story tells you.)

## Sideboard
**Rulebook:** EN p. 3 · DE S. 3

1. **Group Task Track** (left track, spaces 1–10): dice contributed to a group task are placed here.
2. **Threat Track** (right track, 5 spaces): black threat dice are placed here from the top down.
- The **minion column** (minion cards of an encounter) is laid out next to the sideboard (DE: "an die Bedrohungsleiste angelegt").

## Character Card (Stuffy)
**Rulebook:** EN p. 3 · DE S. 3

1. **Name**
2. **Active ability** (DE: *Dauerhafte Fähigkeit*): usable without spending a heart.
3. **Earned abilities** (DE: *Herz-Fähigkeiten*), each marked with a heart: cost 1 heart each time they are used.
4. **Die storage slot**: holds 1 reserved/stored die.
5. **Stuffing storage slot**: holds the stuffy's stuffing tokens (start: 5).
6. **Heart storage slot**: holds the stuffy's hearts.

### The four character cards shown in the rulebook (EN p. 3, 5, 9 · DE S. 3, 5, 9)
| Stuffy (EN / DE) | Active ability | Earned abilities (1 heart each) |
|---|---|---|
| **Theadora** / Theadora | *Versatile / Vielseitig*: once per turn, on Theadora's turn, swap 1 of your attribute dice for an attribute die in the discard pool. | *Resourcefulness / Einfallsreich*: when any player rolls attribute dice, that player may re-roll 1 or more of them. · *Words of Motivation / Motivierende Worte*: any time a player draws dice, that player may return all dice just drawn to the bag and redraw. · *Persevere / Durchhaltewillen*: choose a stuffy; remove 1 or more status cards from it. |
| **Stitch** / Pieks | *Insight / Erfahren*: after rolling dice for Stitch, re-roll 1 or more yellow dice; must keep the second result. | *Outsmart / Clevere Taktik*: before attacking with Stitch, add 1 yellow die to the attack roll. · *Focus / Große Konzentration*: instead of performing a yellow skill test with Stitch, automatically pass it. · *Distract / Ablenkung*: when a **normal** minion is being activated, remove a yellow die stored on Stitch's card and skip that minion's activation. |
| **Flops** / Hopps | *Agile / Flink*: after rolling dice for Flops, re-roll 1 or more green dice; must keep the second result. | *Dodge / Ausweichschritt*: before rolling a green die to defend with Flops, double the roll result. · *Perfect Agility / Perfekte Akrobatin*: instead of a green skill test with Flops, automatically pass. · *Leap / Großer Hopser*: during Flops's turn, choose a space within 4 spaces and in sight of Flops and place Flops there (DE: an *empty* space). |
| **Lumpy** / Stampfi | *Tough / Standhaft*: after rolling dice for Lumpy, re-roll 1 or more blue dice; must keep the second result. | *Protect / Beschützer*: when another stuffy would lose stuffing, Lumpy may lose 1 or more of that stuffing instead. · *Defensive Strike / Abwehrschlag*: before attacking with Lumpy, add 1 blue die to the attack roll. · *Reinforced Stitching / Verstärkte Nähte*: when Lumpy would lose 3 or more stuffing at once, he loses only 2 instead. |

The other two stuffies (**Piggle** / DE *Pinky*, **Lionel** / DE *Leonard*) are not printed in the rulebook; they join later in the campaign.

## Minion Card
**Rulebook:** EN p. 3 · DE S. 3

1. **Name** (e.g. *Crawly* / DE *Krabbler*)
2. **Type**: Normal, Leader or Boss (DE: Normal, Anführer, Obermonster)
3. **Actions**: each action covers a range of die results (e.g. "1-3", "4-6") and shows three values plus optional special text:
   - **Speed** (four-pointed arrow/cross icon) – spaces it moves,
   - **Range** (crosshair icon) – how far it can attack,
   - **Strength** (starburst icon) – damage it deals.
4. **Defense** (number in the grey box at the bottom left): the attack roll needed to defeat it.
5. **Miniature base shape**: helps match card to figure.

Example – **Crawly** (Normal, defense 5):
- 1-3: **Rush** (DE *Losstürmen*): speed 6, range 1, strength 3.
- 4-6: **Clickety** (DE *Krabbel-di-krabb*): speed 3, range 1, strength 4. "After this crawly attacks, move it 3 spaces toward the closest minion start space."

## Discovery Deck
**Rulebook:** EN p. 3 · DE S. 3

- **Do NOT rearrange, shuffle or look through** these cards. They are pre-sorted and split by divider cards into stories ("Story 1", ...).
- Each story tells you to collect that story's discovery cards and set them aside face down without looking. During play you will be told to retrieve specific cards from them.
- After each story, any **discovered** cards are added to their respective decks (items to the item deck, minions to the minion deck, etc.) unless instructed otherwise.
- EN: cards that were **not discovered** are then also added to their respective decks so they're available for future stories. **DE differs:** undiscovered cards go back into the discovery deck (*Abenteuerstapel*). If this comes up, mention both wordings.

## Status Cards
**Rulebook:** EN p. 4 · DE S. 4 (rules: EN p. 13 · DE S. 13)

1. **Name** · 2. **Effect** (often includes a skill test that removes the card)

Example – **Worried** (DE *Besorgt*): If this stuffy has a purple die stored, discard it. This stuffy cannot use or store purple dice. Remove by passing a blue skill test difficulty 4, or by an adjacent stuffy passing a blue skill test difficulty 2.

## Environment Cards
**Rulebook:** EN p. 4 · DE S. 4

1. **Name** · 2. **Effect**. Only used in certain stories (the Storybook sets them up).

Example – **Darkness/Low Visibility** (DE *Dunkelheit/Schlechte Sicht*): The range value of all equipped cards becomes 1. Before a ranged attack, players may spend 1 or more yellow dice to increase that attack's range by the result rolled. A weapon cannot exceed its printed range value.

## Lost Card
**Rulebook:** EN p. 4 · DE S. 4 (rules: EN p. 6 · DE S. 6)

1. **Name** · 2. **Story** (flavor text) · 3. **Effect** (usually a CHOICE with outcomes)

Example – **Ticking Toy Bomb** (DE *Tickende Robo-Bombe*): CHOICE: run or defuse. *Run*: move your stuffy 4 spaces (DE: away, following normal movement rules). *Defuse*: yellow skill test difficulty 3. Pass: remove up to 2 threat dice from the threat track and put all dice from the discard pool back in the bag. Fail (or cannot): at the end of your turn, all stuffies within 3 spaces of this space receive 3 damage.

## Sleep Cards
**Rulebook:** EN p. 4 · DE S. 4 (rules: EN p. 13 · DE S. 13; setup EN p. 5)

Three kinds: **Sleeping** (DE *Ruhiger Schlaf*), **Restless** (DE *Unruhiger Schlaf*), **Waking** (DE *Aufgewacht!*). 10 cards in total, exactly 1 Waking card.

## Dice
**Rulebook:** EN p. 4, 8 · DE S. 4, 8

35 dice in 7 colors:
- **White** = stuffing dice (DE *Wattewürfel*)
- **Black** = threat dice (DE *Bedrohungswürfel*)
- **Attribute dice** (DE *Aktionswürfel*): **red, green, yellow, blue, purple** (DE: purple is called **pink** – *pinke Würfel*).

## Other Components
**Rulebook:** EN p. 4 · DE S. 4

- **Bookmark token** (DE *Lesezeichen*): marks the current bookkeeper.
- **Buttons** (DE *Knöpfe*): currency.
- **Stuffing tokens** (DE *Watteplättchen*): light side = stuffy stuffing; dark side = boss "dark stuffing".
- **Heart tokens** (DE *Herzplättchen*).
- **Objective tokens** (DE *Zielplättchen*), **Lost tokens** (DE *Vergessenen-Plättchen*), **story-specific tokens** (train, door, red wagon…).
- DE S. 5: all other material (figures, environment cards, etc.) is only used in certain stories and appears in that story's setup.
