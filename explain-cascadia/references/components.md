# Components, Object of the Game, Tile Anatomy

Page references: **GS** = German *Cascadia – Im Herzen der Natur* Spielanleitung (KOSMOS 2022, 16 pages), **LM** = German *Cascadia Landmarks* Spielanleitung (KOSMOS 2023, 12 pages). In both PDFs the printed page number equals the PDF page number (page 1 = cover, unnumbered). Only German rulebooks exist for these editions; English terms in this skill are working translations (see `terminology-en-de.md`).

## Object of the game (Spielidee)
**Rulebook:** GS S. 2

- Tile-laying game for **1–4 players** (base game), age 10+, designer Randy Flynn. (GS S. 1)
- You build habitats for the wildlife of the Cascadia region: draft **habitat tiles** (Wildnisplättchen), add them to your own landscape and place **wildlife tokens** (Tiere) on them.
- Each game uses **5 wildlife scoring cards** (Wertungskarten), one per animal species; they show the patterns in which you must place the animals to score.
- At the same time you compete for the **largest connected areas** of each of the five habitats.
- The richest ecosystem (most points) wins.
- GS S. 2 also has region background (pronunciation "KAS-KÄI-DI-A", map) and the game's origin story – flavour only.
- GS S. 4 sidebar: pages up to **S. 9** cover the normal game and are all you need; from **S. 10** on: solo mode, challenges, background on landscape and wildlife.
- GS S. 15 describes the five animals and five habitats of the region (background/flavour only, no rules); GS S. 16 has credits and the turn summary (see `turn.md`).
- The rulebook mentions a free **"Erklär-App"** (rules explanation app) on GS S. 3 (QR code).

## Base game components (Spielmaterial)
**Rulebook:** GS S. 3

| Component | Count / details |
|---|---|
| Starter habitat tiles (Start-Landschaften) | **5** large tiles, each made of **3 hexagonal spaces** (Felder) |
| Habitat tiles (Wildnisplättchen) | **85**, of which **25 with pine-cone symbol** (keystone tiles, "Plättchen mit Zapfensymbol"); habitats: mountain (Gebirge), forest (Wald), prairie (Prärie), wetland (Feuchtgebiet), river (Fluss) |
| Wildlife tokens (Tiere) | **100** printed wooden discs: **20 each** bear (Grizzlybär), elk (Wapiti-Hirsch), salmon (Königslachs), hawk (Rotschwanzbussard), fox (Rotfuchs) |
| Wildlife scoring cards (Wertungskarten) | **21**: **4 each** for bear, elk, salmon, hawk, fox (cards A–D) plus **1** double-sided card for the "Einfache Wertung" (simple scoring) variant |
| Nature tokens (Zapfen = pine cones) | **25** (cones of the Douglas fir) |
| Cloth bag (Stoffbeutel) | 1, for the wildlife tokens |
| Score pad (Wertungsblock) | 1 |

### Score pad layout (from the image, GS S. 3, 9)
- Columns: one per player (name at top).
- Rows: 5 animal rows (bear, elk, salmon, hawk, fox) → **T** (Tiere, total animals); 5 habitat rows (mountain, forest, prairie, wetland, river), each box split by a diagonal: largest-area points **left**, majority bonus **right** → **G** (Gebiete, total habitats); pine cones row; grand total row. (GS S. 8–9)
- Landmarks score pad adds a **landmark row** (mountain-landmark icon) between pine cones and total. (LM S. 8, from image)

## Habitat tile anatomy (Übersicht der Wildnisplättchen)
**Rulebook:** GS S. 8

- Each habitat tile shows **one or two habitat types** and **one, two or three animal symbols** – the species that may be placed on it.
- **Pine-cone tiles (keystone tiles):** always exactly **one habitat** and exactly **one animal species** in the base game. Placing the matching animal there gives you a pine cone.
- Image examples (GS S. 8): half wetland / half river tile allowing salmon, bear or hawk; half mountain / half prairie tile allowing bear or fox; a forest pine-cone tile with an elk symbol.
- **LM differs:** Landmarks adds pine-cone tiles that show exactly one animal but **several habitats**. (LM S. 2)
- Starter habitat tile: 3 connected spaces; animals may be placed on **any of its three spaces**. (GS S. 7)

## Landmarks expansion components
**Rulebook:** LM S. 2–3

| Component | Count / details |
|---|---|
| Habitat tiles | **45**, of which **15 with pine-cone symbol** |
| Starter habitat tiles | **5** |
| Wildlife tokens | **35** (7 each bear, elk, salmon, hawk, fox) |
| Wildlife scoring cards (Tier-Wertungskarten) | **15**: 3 each for bear, elk, salmon, hawk, fox (cards **E, F, G**) |
| Score pad | 1 |
| Pine cones | **15** |
| Wooden landmarks (Landmarken) | **30**: 6 each forest (trees), wetland (reeds), prairie (grass), mountain (peak), river (wave) |
| Landmark scoring cards (Landmarken-Wertungskarten) | **60**: 12 each forest, wetland, prairie, mountain, river (cards lettered **A–L**) |

- Expansion cards and tiles carry a small **mountain symbol** on the front. Card/tile backs may differ slightly in colour from the base game (production). (LM S. 3)
- Expansion: designers Randy Flynn, Molly Johnson, Robert Melvin, Shawn Stankewich; **1–6 players**, age 10+. (LM S. 2)
- New tiles, starter tiles and tokens work exactly like the base game. They may be mixed permanently into the base game; you don't need to sort them out again, whether you play with or without expansion elements. The new tokens go into the same bag. (LM S. 2)
- New tiles may be used in the solo game and are recommended for the expansion's scenarios. (LM S. 2)
- With the expansion: **130 habitat tiles** and **135 wildlife tokens** in total – *derived* (85 + 45, 100 + 35); consistent with the "remove" numbers in the LM S. 2 table.
