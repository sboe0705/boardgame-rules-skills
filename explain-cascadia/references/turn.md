# Gameplay: Overview and a Turn (Spielablauf, Ein Spielzug)

**GS** = base game rulebook (DE), **LM** = Landmarks rulebook (DE). Printed page = PDF page.

---

## Game overview (Spielablauf im Überblick)
**Rulebook:** GS S. 5

- Start player (last to see one of the animals in the wild, or random), then clockwise until the game ends.
- On your turn choose one of the **4 combinations** (habitat tile + wildlife token) and place both in your **landscape** (Landschaft = your starter tile plus all tiles and tokens you add).
- End your turn by revealing a new tile from a stack and drawing a new token from the bag → 4 combinations again.
- The game ends when a tile must be revealed but all stacks are empty: that is after **every player has had exactly 20 turns**. Then final scoring; most points wins.

## The four steps of a turn
**Rulebook:** GS S. 6 (summary also GS S. 16 "Ein Spielzug im Überblick")

1. Check for overpopulation (Auf Überbevölkerung prüfen)
2. Take a habitat tile and a wildlife token (Wildnisplättchen und Tier nehmen)
3. Place tile and token in your landscape (… in Landschaft platzieren)
4. Refill the display (Auslage auffüllen)

## Step 1 – Check for overpopulation
**Rulebook:** GS S. 6

At the start of each turn there are 4 tiles and 4 tokens, arranged as 4 combinations (A–D in the image).

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| **All 4 tokens the same species** | You **must** replace all four: set them aside, draw 4 new tokens one by one and put them next to the tiles in order. If that again gives 4 of a kind, replace again. |
| **Exactly 3 tokens the same** | You **may** replace exactly those three: set them aside, draw 3 new tokens one by one, place them in order next to the tiles. At most **once per turn**. |

- At the end of this step, mix all set-aside tokens back into the bag. (GS S. 6)
- Image captions: "Überbevölkerung: 4 gleiche Tiere müssen ausgetauscht werden" / "3 gleiche Tiere dürfen ausgetauscht werden".

## Step 2 – Take a tile and a token
**Rulebook:** GS S. 6

- Take **one** combination of one tile and one token – normally one of the displayed pairs. Then go to step 3.
- **Before** taking a combination you may **spend 1 pine cone** to either:
  - take **any one** of the 4 tiles and **any one** of the 4 tokens (instead of a displayed pair), **OR**
  - **replace any number of tokens** with new ones from the bag; then check for overpopulation again.
- Spent pine cones return to the supply.
- You may replace tokens several times in a turn as long as you spend a pine cone each time, but you take only **one tile and one token** in total.
- Reminder box (GS S. 8): same two uses; each remaining pine cone is worth **1 point** at game end.
- *Not stated:* what happens to tokens replaced with a pine cone. By analogy with step 1 they presumably go back into the bag (*interpretation*).

## Step 3 – Place tile and token
**Rulebook:** GS S. 7

You place both in your landscape, in either order.

### Placing the habitat tile (mandatory)
- It must be **adjacent** to your existing landscape: at least one edge touches an edge of a placed tile (your starter tile counts).
- It may **not** be placed on top of another tile.
- You may **rotate** it freely.
- Once placed, a tile can never be moved.
- *Note:* habitats of adjacent tiles **don't have to match**; but large connected areas score more at the end.
- Image: legal spaces (ticks) around the landscape, an illegal space not touching it (cross).

### Placing the wildlife token (optional)
- You may place it on **any** of your habitat tiles – the one just placed or an earlier one – if:
  - the tile has **no token yet** (never more than one token per tile), and
  - the tile **shows that animal** (each tile shows 1–3 animals).
- Your starter tile is 3 connected spaces; you may place a token on each of its spaces. (GS S. 7 note)
- If you can't or don't want to place it (no free tile with that symbol), return it to the **bag**.
- Placing an animal on a **pine-cone tile** earns you **1 pine cone** from the supply.
- Image: a fox may go on free tiles showing a fox (ticks), not on tiles without a fox symbol or already occupied (crosses).
- **LM:** a tile with a landmark can no longer receive a token; with the Landmarks module you place the landmark on the tile just placed and may put the token elsewhere or return it to the bag (see `landmarks.md`). (LM S. 7)

## Step 4 – Refill the display
**Rulebook:** GS S. 7

- Fill the empty slot: reveal a new tile from any stack and put it in the gap, then draw a token from the bag and put it in the gap → 4 combinations again.
- *Note:* don't shift the remaining tiles/tokens; put new ones straight into the empty places.
- **Solo differs:** before refilling, remove the rightmost tile and token from the game and shift the rest right (see `variants-solo.md`). (GS S. 10)

## End of game trigger
**Rulebook:** GS S. 5, 8

- The game ends as soon as you can't refill the display with a tile because all stacks are empty; everyone has had 20 turns. Go to final scoring (`scoring.md`).
- **LM faster game:** 15 turns each plus 5 extra actions (LM S. 4).
