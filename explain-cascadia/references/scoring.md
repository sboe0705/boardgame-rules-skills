# End of Game and Final Scoring (Spielende & Wertung)

**GS** = base game rulebook (DE), **LM** = Landmarks rulebook (DE). Printed page = PDF page.

---

## Scoring categories
**Rulebook:** GS S. 8

The game ends when the display can no longer be refilled with a tile (all stacks empty); everyone has had 20 turns. Score in four categories and note them on the score pad:

1. **Wildlife** according to the scoring cards (Tiere gemäß Wertungskarten)
2. **Largest area of each habitat** (Größtes Gebiet jeder Wildnisart)
3. **Comparison of largest areas** – majority bonus (Vergleich der größten Gebiete)
4. **Remaining pine cones** (Übrige Zapfen)
5. **LM adds:** landmark scoring cards (row with the landmark icon), if playing with the Landmarks module. (LM S. 8)

### 1. Wildlife
**Rulebook:** GS S. 8

- For each species (bear, elk, salmon, hawk, fox) score as shown on that species' scoring card. Card details: `wildlife-scoring-cards.md` (GS S. 11, LM S. 5).

### 2. Largest area of each habitat
**Rulebook:** GS S. 8

- For each habitat (mountain, forest, prairie, wetland, river) find your **largest connected area**; score **1 point per tile** in it.
- An **area** (Gebiet) is one or more connected tiles of that habitat. LM S. 7 repeats: one or more connected tiles of the same habitat.
- Enter these points **left of the diagonal** in that habitat's box.
- Example: you have a forest area of 3 and one of 4 → largest forest area is 4 → **4 points**.
- *Derived from the tile anatomy:* a dual-habitat tile can belong to an area of each of its habitats; which edges connect is decided by the habitat shown on the touching edges (the rulebook shows this only in pictures, not in words – *interpretation*).

### 3. Comparison of largest areas (majority bonus)
**Rulebook:** GS S. 8–9

Compare the habitat points just entered; whoever has the LARGEST area of each habitat gets bonus points. Enter them **right of the diagonal**.

| Player count | Bonus |
|---|---|
| **3 or 4 players** | Largest **3**, second largest **1**. Two tied for largest: **2 each**. Three or more tied for largest: **1 each**. In both tie cases no points for second largest. A tie for second largest **never** scores. |
| **2 players** | Largest **2**, no points for second. Tie: **1 each**. |
| **Solo** | **2** bonus points for every habitat in which you have an area of **at least 7**. |
| 5–6 players (LM) | **Not covered** by either rulebook. |

### 4. Remaining pine cones
**Rulebook:** GS S. 8

- **1 point** per remaining pine cone.

## Winner and tiebreak
**Rulebook:** GS S. 8

- Add everything up; most points wins.
- Tie: whoever has **more pine cones left** wins. Still tied: share the victory (or play another game).

## Detailed scoring example (3 players)
**Rulebook:** GS S. 9

Cards used: Bear A, Elk A, Salmon A, Hawk A, Fox A. Veronika's final score:

| Category | Veronika's result |
|---|---|
| Bear (A) | two bear pairs → **11** |
| Elk (A) | a line of 2 elk and a line of 3 elk → **14** (5 + 9) |
| Salmon (A) | a run of 4 salmon → **12** |
| Hawk (A) | four solitary hawks → **11** |
| Fox (A) | three foxes → **11** (3 + 5 + 3) |
| Mountain | 6 connected → 6 points; tied with Ute → **+2** bonus each |
| Forest | 4 connected → 4; three-way tie → **+1** |
| Prairie | 7 connected → 7; less than Ute, more than Tim → **+1** (second largest) |
| Wetland | 8 connected → 8; more than both others → **+3** |
| River | 5 connected → 5; smallest → no bonus |
| Pine cones | 2 left → **2** |
| **Total** | **98** (narrow win) |

## Landmarks scoring
**Rulebook:** LM S. 8

- Score as usual, then add the points from the landmark scoring cards you own; each card says how. Enter them in the **landmark row**.
- *Note:* some landmark cards give no points but advantages for other scoring (e.g. for single areas) – don't forget them during final scoring.
- Card explanations and the scoring example: `landmarks.md`.
