# Setup (Spielvorbereitung)

**GS** = base game rulebook (DE), **LM** = Landmarks rulebook (DE). Printed page = PDF page.

---

## Base game setup (7 steps)
**Rulebook:** GS S. 4 (setup image GS S. 5)

1. Put all **wildlife tokens** in the bag and mix well.
2. Prepare **habitat tiles**: count out random tiles by player count. **Important:** don't look at the fronts, so nobody knows which tiles are in the game.
   | Players | Tiles used | Removed (of 85) |
   |---|---|---|
   | 2 | **43** | 42 |
   | 3 | **63** | 22 |
   | 4 | **83** | 2 |
   - Rule: always **20 tiles per player + 3**.
   - Shuffle them and form any number of face-down stacks (number of stacks doesn't matter). Return the rest to the box unseen.
3. Take **5 wildlife scoring cards**, one per species.
   - First games: use the five cards marked **A** (bottom right). Lay them face up at the edge of the play area; return the others.
   - After a few games: draw a random card per species, or choose your favourites.
   - All cards are explained on GS S. 11 (see `wildlife-scoring-cards.md`).
   - Sidebar: for **younger children** use the simple scoring card instead (one side, only that card) – see `variants-solo.md`. (GS S. 4, 10)
4. Give each player a random **starter habitat tile** (large, 3 spaces), face up in front of them. Return the rest.
5. Reveal **4 habitat tiles** from the stacks and lay them in a row.
6. Draw **4 wildlife tokens** one at a time and put one next to each revealed tile → **4 combinations** (tile + token), the display (Auslage).
7. Put the **pine cones** out as a general supply.

**Solo:** count out tiles as for 2 players (43); put the stacks to the left of the display. (GS S. 10)

## Start player
**Rulebook:** GS S. 5

- Whoever most recently saw one of the game's animals in the wild starts; or choose randomly. Play continues clockwise.

## Tile counts with the Landmarks expansion
**Rulebook:** LM S. 2

Total tiles in the box with the expansion: 130 (*derived*).

| Players | Standard | With the Landmarks module (see `landmarks.md`) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 43 (remove 87) | 44 (remove 86) – incl. 1 extra pine-cone tile |
| 2 | 43 (remove 87) | 45 (remove 85) – incl. 2 extra pine-cone tiles |
| 3 | 63 (remove 67) | 66 (remove 64) – incl. 3 extra pine-cone tiles |
| 4 | 83 (remove 47) | 87 (remove 43) – incl. 4 extra pine-cone tiles |
| 5 | 103 (remove 27) | 108 (remove 22) – incl. 5 extra pine-cone tiles |
| 6 | 123 (remove 7) | 129 (remove 1) – incl. 6 extra pine-cone tiles |

- The extra tiles in the Landmarks column are the ones each player takes as an opening pine-cone tile during Landmarks setup. (*Interpretation* of "enthält N zusätzliche Plättchen mit Zapfensymbol" together with LM S. 6.)
- With the expansion the game supports **up to 6 players** (5 starter tiles + 5 new = 10). For 5–6 players the rulebook recommends the **faster game rules** (LM S. 4, see `variants-solo.md`), but standard rules work for any player count.
- **Gap:** neither rulebook states the habitat majority bonus for **5–6 players**; GS S. 9 only covers solo, 2 and 3–4 players. Applying the 3–4 player rule is an *interpretation*.
