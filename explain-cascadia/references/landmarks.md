# Landmarks Module (Die Landmarken) – Cascadia Landmarks expansion

**LM** = German *Cascadia Landmarks* rulebook (KOSMOS 2023). Printed page = PDF page.

The expansion contains (1) new tiles, starter tiles and tokens (usable in any game, up to 6 players, LM S. 2), (2) wildlife scoring cards E–G (`wildlife-scoring-cards.md`, LM S. 5), (3) faster game rules (`variants-solo.md`, LM S. 4), and (4) the **Landmarks module** described here (LM S. 6–8, summary LM S. 12).

Setup, play and final scoring follow the base game with the changes below. Use the tile count from the "Landmarks module" column on LM S. 2 (see `setup.md`).

---

## Setup changes
**Rulebook:** LM S. 6

1. **Opening pine-cone tile:** after dealing starter tiles, reveal tiles from those in play until as many **pine-cone tiles** are face up as there are players.
   - Starting with the player **to the right of the start player** and going **counterclockwise**, each player chooses one pine-cone tile and places it in their landscape following the normal rules.
   - **Solo:** no choice – take the first pine-cone tile you reveal.
   - Shuffle the other revealed tiles back into the supply and continue normal setup.
   - Example (3 players): Tim takes the prairie pine-cone tile and adds it to his starter tile.
   - *Not stated:* whether a token is placed with this tile – the rule mentions only the tile (no token is drawn).
2. **Landmark scoring cards:** sort them by habitat (forest, prairie, mountain, river, wetland) into 5 stacks, shuffle each and reveal **as many cards as there are players** from each. **Solo:** reveal **2** per stack. Lay them near the wildlife scoring cards.
3. **Landmark pieces:** put landmarks of each habitat next to the matching landmark cards – **as many per habitat as there are players**.
- Image (3 players): 3 face-up cards per habitat, 3 landmarks each.

## Play changes
**Rulebook:** LM S. 7 (summary LM S. 12)

- When placing a tile **enlarges a connected area of one habitat to size 5 or more**, you **may** take a landmark of that habitat and put it **on the tile just placed**.
  - It must go on the tile you just placed, not on another tile of that area.
  - **No animal** may ever be placed on a tile with a landmark. Each landmark reduces your options for placing animals later.
  - You may take and place **only one landmark per habitat** (max. 5 landmarks per player).
  - A landmark on a **pine-cone tile gives no pine cone**.
- After placing the landmark, **choose one of the landmark scoring cards** (of that habitat – *follows from the example and the "one card per landmark" maximum*) and place it next to your landscape. It scores at game end for met conditions or gives other advantages (e.g. enlarging an area). Max. **5 landmarks and 5 landmark cards** per player.
- *Note:* after putting a landmark on the tile, you may place the token on another tile, or return it to the bag.
- An "area" is one or more connected tiles of the same habitat.
- Example: Aniko enlarges her prairie area to 5, puts a prairie landmark on the tile just placed and picks a prairie card ("Prärie", 3 points). She may place her animal on another free tile, but not on the landmark tile.
- *Derived:* landmarks and cards are limited (one per player per habitat); the scenario rule on LM S. 9 ("in scenarios 11–20 the same landmark card may be chosen by several players even if already chosen") implies that **normally a chosen card is no longer available** to others.
- *Not stated:* whether you may take a landmark when your area was already 5+ before (the rule says "enlarge to size 5 or more", so any placement that grows an area that ends at 5+ seems to qualify – *interpretation*).

## Landmark scoring
**Rulebook:** LM S. 8

- Score normally, then add the points of your landmark cards in the **landmark row**. Some cards give advantages instead of points – remember them during scoring.

### Landmark scoring card types (12 per habitat, letters A–L)
| Cards | Rule |
|---|---|
| **A–E** | Points for animals "on" a habitat: an animal is on a habitat if at least part of its tile shows that habitat. Examples: "Bär im Wald" (A): 1 per bear on forest; "Lachs auf Prärie" (C): 1 per salmon on prairie. |
| **F** | Pine-cone tiles: a pine-cone tile counts as the required habitat if at least part of it shows it. Example "Bewohnte Wildnis": 3 points per pair of **occupied wetland pine-cone tiles**. |
| **G–H** | Points for **each area** of the habitat. **G:** areas of size 1 count too (example "Gebiete": 1 per prairie / mountain area). **H:** an area needs at least 2 tiles. |
| **I–J** | **No points** but advantages when scoring areas of that habitat; they count for determining that habitat's area size, but **not** as points for the landmark goals of scenarios (LM S. 9–10). Examples: "Zwei Gebiete" (I): combine your two forest areas (largest + another, per the example) into one total; "Vergrößertes Gebiet" (J): the card shows a 3-tile + 2-tile area added together for the largest mountain area (card text not legible in the rulebook image). |
| **K** | "Begrenzte Gebiete": card shows **6** base points, **−1 per area** of that habitat. Max. 5 points, since every landscape has at least one area of the habitat. With 6+ areas you get **0**, never negative points. |
| **L** | No condition: **3 points** (card shows only the habitat name, e.g. "Fluss", "Prärie"). |

- Card names seen in setup images include "Fuchs auf Prärie", "Einzigartige Tierwelt", "Große Gebiete", "Lachs im Feuchtgebiet", "Fuchs & Lachs", "Bär im Fluss", "Bär & Hirsch" (low-resolution images; exact text only on the cards).

### Scoring example
**Rulebook:** LM S. 8

- "Zwei Gebiete" (forest, I): combines forest areas of 6 and 2 into **8** – entered in the **habitat** category (forest row), not as landmark points.
- "Gebiete" (mountain, G): 5 mountain areas → **5**.
- "Lachs auf Prärie" (C): 4 salmon on prairie tiles → **4**.
- "Bewohnte Wildnis" (wetland, F): one pair of occupied wetland pine-cone tiles → **3**.
- Landmark row: 5 + 4 + 3 = **12**. Score pad from the image: player SS total **111** (animals 59, habitats 37, cones 3, landmarks 12); player RF 113.
