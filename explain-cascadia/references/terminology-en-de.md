# Terminology: German (official KOSMOS) ↔ English

The German terms are the **official** terms from the KOSMOS rulebooks (base game 2022, Landmarks 2023) – use them when answering in German. Only German rulebooks were available; the English terms are the working terms used in this skill (common names of the English AEG edition from general usage, **not verified against an English rulebook**). Answer in English with these, giving the German term in brackets if the asker plays the German edition.
