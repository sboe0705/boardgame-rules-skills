# Solo Game, Simple Scoring, Faster Game, Rule Variants

**GS** = base game rulebook (DE), **LM** = Landmarks rulebook (DE). Printed page = PDF page.

---

## Solo game (Das Solo-Spiel)
**Rulebook:** GS S. 10

- **Setup:** almost unchanged. Count out tiles as for 2 players (**43**). Put the tile stacks to the **left** of the display.
- **Play:** unchanged except: **before refilling** the display on your turn, remove the **rightmost** tile and the **rightmost** token of the display from the game. Then shift the remaining two tiles and tokens to the right (this can create new combinations). Finally refill the two empty spaces as usual.
- Example: you spend a pine cone to take the forest tile and a matching elk (separately). After placing both, remove the rightmost tile and token (here the mountain/wetland tile and the bear) from the game; shift the other two tiles and tokens right; refill the two empty spaces.
- **Scoring:** normal, except no area comparison: **2 bonus points per habitat with an area of size 7+**. (GS S. 9–10)
- **Rating table:**
  | Points | Rating (DE) |
  |---|---|
  | 60+ | Ein guter Anfang (a good start) |
  | 70+ | Es wird :-) (getting there) |
  | 80+ | Weiter so (keep going) |
  | 90+ | Sehr gut (very good) |
  | 100+ | Ausgezeichnet (excellent) |
  | 110+ | Den Gipfel erreicht! (summit reached) |
- For a special challenge use the **scenarios** on GS S. 13 (see `challenges.md`).
- **LM solo:** with the Landmarks module, take the first revealed pine-cone tile (no choice) and reveal **2** landmark cards per habitat (LM S. 6). Scenarios of Landmarks also work solo (LM S. 9). The solo game is mentioned for the normal-game challenges with landmarks (LM S. 11). Total with LM tiles: 43 (44 with Landmarks module) (LM S. 2).

## Simple scoring (Einfache Wertung)
**Rulebook:** GS S. 10 (sidebar GS S. 4)

- One double-sided scoring card for an especially easy start; all species are scored **the same way**, so young children can play.
- Choose the **Einsteiger** (beginner) or **Fortgeschrittene** (advanced) side. In setup step 3 lay out **only this card** with that side up; return the others.
- To simplify habitat scoring too, you may simply **skip the comparison of largest areas** (majority bonus).
- **Beginner side:** at game end, score each **group of the same species**; larger groups score more. Groups of 1 (single animals), 2 and 3 (or more): size 1 → **2**, size 2 → **5**, size 3+ → **9**. Groups of any shape; groups of the same species can't be adjacent.
  - Example: a 2-fox group (5), a 3-salmon group (9), a 2-elk group (5), three single hawks (3 × 2) and two single bears (2 × 2) → **29 points**.
- **Advanced side:** works the same, but only groups of size **2, 3 and 4+** score: 2 → **5**, 3 → **8**, 4+ → **12** (values from the card image, GS S. 4).

## Faster game (Regeln für ein schnelleres Spiel) – Landmarks
**Rulebook:** LM S. 4

Standard rules suit every player count; for **5–6 players** or a generally faster game these rules are recommended.

**Setup**
1. From the bag take **as many tokens of each of the 5 species as there are players**.
2. Each player takes **one token of each species** and lays them side by side at the edge of their area.
3. From the tiles counted for the player count (LM S. 2), deal each player **5 tiles** face down; turn them up and put one next to each of your tokens → **5 random combinations** with bear, elk, salmon, hawk and fox: your **personal supply**.

**Play** – as usual, with this change:
- **Between your turns**, while others are taking theirs, you may take an **extra action**: choose a tile/token combination from your personal supply and place it in your landscape. **Once per round**, **5 times per game** in total.
- The personal supply is **not refilled**. But you may **spend 1 pine cone to swap 2 tokens of your choice** in your supply before taking the extra action.
- *Note:* tiles and tokens left in your personal supply at game end can't be used anymore.
- Everyone has exactly **15 turns** instead of 20, plus 5 extra actions → still 20 tiles and 20 tokens each. The extra actions shorten play, work especially well with 5–6 players, and with fewer players guarantee access to each animal once when you need it.
- *Derived:* the 5 personal tiles come out of the LM S. 2 count (20 per player + 3), leaving 15 per player + 3 in the stacks – which matches 15 turns each.

## Rule variants (Herausforderungen – Regel-Variante)
**Rulebook:** GS S. 14

Part of the challenge mode (choose exactly one per game; the winner checks it off – see `challenges.md`). Not for solo play.

1. When placing a tile, at least **one habitat must match** (an adjacent edge).
2. When placing a tile, **identical habitats must not touch** (so the area scoring is dropped at the end).
3. Area scoring change: **every area of size 3** scores exactly **3** points; all other areas score nothing.
4. Area scoring change: only areas of size **5+** score (normal rules).
5. The display has only **three combinations** (one fewer).
6. Play with **two sets** of scoring cards (2 × 5). At the end, each player decides which set they're scored with.
7. **No starter tiles:** each player starts with **two habitat tiles**, drawn randomly from those removed during setup.
8. You must always spend **two pine cones** for one of the extra pine-cone actions.
9. Animals may **not** be placed on the tile you just placed.
10. **Secret scoring cards:** in setup each player secretly receives one of the five randomly chosen scoring cards; keep it secret. Undistributed cards are laid out face up as usual. As soon as someone spends a pine cone, their secret card is revealed and added to the others.
