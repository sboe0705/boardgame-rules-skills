# Setup (Spielaufbau)

Page conventions: see `components.md`. SCO = Scotland rulebook, IRL = Ireland rulebook.

---

## Scotland setup
**Rulebook:** DE SCO S. 2

1. Put the board in the middle of the table, **Scotland side up**.
2. Shuffle the **Scotland mission cards** into 1 deck and put it on its place on the board.
3. Each player: choose 1 colour and put your point marker on the **0** space of the score track.
4. Each player: take all castles and cathedrals of your colour. Put your 100 point marker in front of you, **back side up**.
5. **2 players only:** put **32 tiles of any unused colour face down** on the marked spaces (shown with a yellow hexagon icon; the image shows where they are on the map).
6. Tiles by player count:
   - **2 or 3 players:** take all tiles of your colour.
   - **4 players:** take all tiles of your colour, then each player returns to the box: **3 energy facilities, 3 food facilities and 1 settlement each with 2, 3 and 4 settlement symbols** (9 tiles).
7. Each player: put all your tiles face down in a pile in front of you and mix them well.
8. Each player: draw **2** of your tiles, look at them and put them **face down next to your 200/300 point marker**. Don't show them to the others. These 2 tiles are **not used** this game, but you may look at them at any time. (You need the 200/300 marker only once you pass 200/300 points.) All remaining tiles of your colour are your **personal supply (Vorrat)**.
9. Each player: draw **1 tile** from your personal supply into your hand. Keep tiles in your hand secret.
10. Whoever visited Scotland most recently starts (or choose at random).

Images (SCO S. 2): 8 cathedrals and 12 castles per player; 32 neutral tiles for 2 players.

### Tiles in play (follows from the rules, not stated as numbers)
| Players | Tiles per colour | Set aside | Personal supply incl. first hand tile |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2–3 | 36 | 2 | 34 |
| 4 | 27 (9 food, 9 energy, 9 settlements) | 2 | 25 |

## Ireland setup
**Rulebook:** DE IRL S. 2

Steps that differ from Scotland are highlighted in yellow in the rulebook; marked **(differs)** here.

1. **(differs)** Board in the middle, **Ireland side up**.
2. **(differs)** Shuffle the **Ireland mission cards** and reveal **8 cards in a row above the board**. The rest form a face-down deck on its place on the board.
3. **(differs)** Shuffle the **round tower bonus markers** face down, put 1 at random on each round tower space (yellow spaces with a round tower picture) and turn them face up.
4. Each player: choose a colour, point marker on 0.
5. Each player: take all castles and cathedrals of your colour; 100 point marker back side up in front of you.
6. **(differs)** Each player: put the **doubling marker** of your colour in front of you.
7. **(differs)** **2 players only:** put **24 tiles** of any unused colour face down on the marked spaces.
8. Tiles by player count: same as Scotland (2–3 players all tiles; 4 players each return 3 energy facilities, 3 food facilities and 1 settlement each with 2, 3 and 4 symbols).
9. Each player: tiles face down in a pile, mix well.
10. **(differs)** Each player: draw **4** of your tiles, look at them, **mix them again** and put them face down next to your 200/300 point marker. Don't show them to the others. All remaining tiles are your personal supply.
    - **Note:** unlike in Scotland you may **not** look at these face-down tiles again.
    - These 4 tiles are used by the round tower bonus "Place a 2nd tile" (IRL S. 4).
11. Each player: draw 1 tile into your hand, keep it secret.
12. Whoever visited Ireland most recently starts (or choose at random).

Images (IRL S. 2): 14 round tower bonus markers, 24 neutral tiles for 2 players, 8 cathedrals, 12 castles.

### Tiles in play in Ireland (follows from the rules)
| Players | Tiles per colour | Set aside | Personal supply incl. first hand tile |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2–3 | 36 | 4 | 32 |
| 4 | 27 | 4 | 23 |
