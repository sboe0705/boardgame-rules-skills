# Scotland mission cards (Missionskarten Schottland)

Source: player aid "Kartenerklärungen Schottland" (Aid front and back, unnumbered) plus SCO S. 4. Page conventions: see `components.md`.

How you get mission cards (cathedrals): see `turn.md`. They are scored in the final scoring (`end-of-game.md`).

---

## General rules for mission cards
**Rulebook:** DE Aid front · SCO S. 4

- 32 Scotland mission cards (SCO S. 1). Every card shown is worth **5 points**, except the Cathedral Bonus (**3 points**).
- Each of your mission cards scores **only for you**. If another player beats you at its condition, they get nothing for it – and neither do you.
- "**Most**" (die meisten) always means compared with the **other players**, not the majority of the spaces on the board.
- **A tie is enough:** the cards say "(Gleichstand der meisten / größten / längsten genügt)" – you fulfil the card if you are tied for first (also SCO S. 4, "Wichtig").
- The examples in the player aid are always from Pink's point of view.

## Card types
**Rulebook:** DE Aid front (all but the last two) · Aid back (Cathedral Bonus, Most Cathedrals/Castles)

| Card (German title) | Condition | Points |
|---|---|---|
| Food Facility Groups (Nahrungs-Anlagen-Gruppen) | Have the most groups of 3 or more food facilities | 5 |
| Energy Facility Groups (Energie-Anlagen-Gruppen) | Have the most groups of 3 or more energy facilities | 5 |
| Largest Food Facility (Größte Nahrungs-Anlage) | Have the largest connected group of food facilities | 5 |
| Largest Energy Facility (Größte Energie-Anlage) | Have the largest connected group of energy facilities | 5 |
| Presence in Medium Settlements (Präsenz in mittleren Siedlungen) | Have at least 1 settlement tile in the most medium settlements | 5 |
| Presence in Large Settlements (Präsenz in großen Siedlungen) | Have at least 1 settlement tile in the most large settlements | 5 |
| Ownership of Small Settlements (Besitz von kleinen Siedlungen) | Have fully occupied the most small settlements – at least 1 | 5 |
| Ownership of Medium Settlements (Besitz von mittleren Siedlungen) | Have occupied all spaces in the most medium settlements – at least 1 | 5 |
| Longest Coastline (Längste Küstenlinie) | Occupy the longest chain of connected spaces along the coast | 5 |
| Most Harbours (Meiste Häfen) | Occupy the most spaces with a harbour | 5 |
| English Border (Englische Grenze) | Occupy the most spaces adjacent to England | 5 |
| Castle … (e.g. Burg Blair) | Occupy the most spaces adjacent to the named castle | 5 |
| Cathedral … (e.g. Kathedrale Inverness) | Occupy the most spaces adjacent to the named cathedral | 5 |
| Most Cathedrals (Meiste Kathedralen) | Have the most cathedrals on the board | 5 |
| Most Castles (Meiste Burgen) | Have the most castles on the board | 5 |
| Cathedral Bonus (Kathedralen-Bonus) | None: 3 victory points | 3 |

The player aid shows several different "Castle …" and "Cathedral …" cards fanned out (one per named place); it does not list how many copies of each card type the deck holds.

## Explanations and examples

### Facility groups
**Rulebook:** DE Aid front
- Try to form as many groups of 3 or more facilities as possible. A group of 6 does **not** count as 2 groups of 3.
- Example: Pink has 2 groups of at least 3 facilities; nobody has more than 2 → Pink gets 5 points.

### Largest facility
**Rulebook:** DE Aid front
- Try to form the largest connected group of one facility type.
- Example: Pink gets nothing for Largest Energy Facility because Pink's group has only 4 tiles. Blue has a group of 5 but does not hold the card, so Blue gets nothing either.

### Presence in settlements
**Rulebook:** DE Aid front
- 1 tile in a settlement is enough for presence; you don't need the whole settlement. Spreading settlement tiles widely pays off.
- Be present in more settlements (of the named size) than any other player. The number of settlement symbols is irrelevant.
- Example: Pink is present in all 4 large settlements, Blue and Orange only in 2 → Pink gets 5 points.

### Ownership of settlements
**Rulebook:** DE Aid front
- You must have occupied **every space** of a settlement (1 space is not enough). Fully occupy more settlements than any other player.
- You need at least 1 such settlement: **0 settlements is not a tie**.
- The number of settlement symbols is irrelevant.
- Example: Pink and Blue each occupy all spaces in 2 medium settlements, Orange in 1 → Pink is part of the tie and gets 5 points.

### Longest coastline
**Rulebook:** DE Aid front
- Have the longest connected chain of adjacent tiles along the coast. **Castles and cathedrals interrupt** chains.
- Example: Pink has the longest chain of connected spaces along the coast (4) and gets 5 points. Orange also has 4 adjacent tiles at Castle Kilberry, but one connection runs over land, so that chain is only 3 long along the coast.

### Most harbours
**Rulebook:** DE Aid front
- Harbours are recognised by the "+1" bonus point.
- Example: Blue and Pink occupy 3 harbours each, Orange 2 → Pink is tied for most and gets 5 points.

### English border
**Rulebook:** DE Aid front
- Occupy as many spaces as possible that border England at the bottom right. Only the **number of tiles** counts; they need not be adjacent to each other. The castle (there) does not count.
- Example: Pink and Blue each occupy 2 spaces adjacent to England → Pink gets 5 points.

### Tiles around a cathedral / castle
**Rulebook:** DE Aid front
- Be (tied for) the player with the most tiles around the named castle or cathedral. **Who owns the castle and the type of tiles are irrelevant.**
- Example (castle): although the castle belongs to Blue (castle majority rule), Pink is tied for the number of tiles and gets 5 points.
- Example (cathedral): all players are tied → Pink gets 5 points.

### Cathedral Bonus
**Rulebook:** DE Aid back · SCO S. 4
- You simply get 3 points per Cathedral Bonus card, no matter how many cathedrals you placed. Nothing else to fulfil.

### Most cathedrals / castles
**Rulebook:** DE Aid back
- Example: Blue, Orange and Pink each have 7 castles on the board → Pink is part of the tie and gets 5 points.
