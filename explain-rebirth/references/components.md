# Object of the game and components (Spielziel, Spielmaterial)

Sources: German Frosted Games rulebooks v1.1. **SCO** = Scotland rulebook (4 pages, printed page = PDF page). **IRL** = Ireland rulebook (4 pages; the PDF is in reverse order: PDF page 1 = printed S. 4 … PDF page 4 = printed S. 1; printed pages are cited). **Aid** = player aid PDF "Spielhilfe" (2 unnumbered sides: front, back).

English terms are working translations (see `terminology-en-de.md`).

---

## The two variants
**Rulebook:** DE SCO S. 1 · IRL S. 1

- The game board is double-sided: **Scotland** on one side, **Ireland** on the other. Each side is its own variant with its own rulebook.
- **First game:** always play the Scotland variant first (SCO S. 1).
- The Ireland variant plays similarly but **assumes you have played Scotland**; its rulebook only describes the differences and refers back to the Scotland rulebook (IRL S. 1).
- Player count: the setup rules cover 2, 3 and 4 players (SCO S. 2, IRL S. 2).

## Object of the game
**Rulebook:** DE SCO S. 2 · IRL S. 2

- **Scotland:** score the most points by placing your tiles to build energy and food facilities and found settlements. At the end of the game you also score missions collected at cathedrals and the castles you control.
- **Ireland:** score the most points with energy and food facilities and settlements. Controlling castles, building on wind collectors and mission cards give additional points.
- The player with the most points at the end wins.

## Board anatomy – Scotland
**Rulebook:** DE SCO S. 2–4 (text and images)

- Hexagonal spaces. The legend printed on the board names: energy facility space (lightning bolt), food facility space (leaves), energy/food facility space (**empty space**, no symbol), castle, cathedral, settlement (image, SCO S. 2).
- **Settlement spaces** show a house symbol and are grouped into framed areas: small settlement = 1 space with a single frame, medium = 2 spaces with a double frame, large = 3 spaces with a triple frame (SCO S. 3).
- **Harbours (Häfen):** some settlement spaces have a harbour, recognisable by a "+1" bonus point marker (SCO S. 3, Aid front).
- **Castle spaces (Burgfelder)** and **cathedral spaces (Kathedralenfelder)** are named spaces (e.g. Blair, Inverness) that never hold tiles; castles and cathedrals are placed on them. There are **8 cathedral spaces** (SCO S. 4).
- **Water** spaces cannot be built on (SCO S. 2).
- A **score track** runs around the edge of the board, starting at 0 (SCO S. 2).
- A marked place for the mission card deck (SCO S. 2).
- Spaces marked for the 2-player game (shown in the rulebook as yellow hexagon icons): 32 spaces (SCO S. 2).
- Edinburgh Castle and Stirling Castle are marked with white shamrock symbols; they are the tie-breakers (SCO S. 4).
- England lies at the bottom right of the map (Aid front, "Englische Grenze").

## Board anatomy – Ireland
**Rulebook:** DE IRL S. 2–4

- Same space types as Scotland, plus:
- **Round tower spaces (Rundturmfelder):** yellow spaces with the picture of a round tower; each gets 1 round tower bonus marker in setup (IRL S. 2).
- **Wind collectors (Windsammler):** "+1" spaces that replace the Scotland harbours (IRL S. 3).
- **1 extra-large settlement** of 4 spaces with a quadruple frame (IRL S. 3). The board prints the settlement values: 4 spaces 12/8/5/0, 3 spaces 8/5/0, 2 spaces 5/3 (image, IRL S. 2).
- 24 marked spaces for the 2-player game (IRL S. 2).
- Blarney Castle and the round tower on the Hill of Tara are marked with white shamrock symbols (tie-breakers, IRL S. 4).

## Scoring overview card (Wertungsübersicht)
**Rulebook:** DE SCO S. 1 (image, small print)

Title "Gleichstände lösen" (resolving ties):

| Section | 1st | 2nd |
|---|---|---|
| Castles | Majority of energy and food facilities | Active player loses the tie |
| Settlements | Active player loses the tie | Others: turn order wins |
| End of game – Scotland | Control of Edinburgh Castle | Control of Stirling Castle |
| End of game – Ireland | Control of Blarney Castle | Most adjacent spaces to the Hill of Tara occupied |

## Point markers
**Rulebook:** DE SCO S. 2, 4

- Your point marker moves along the score track.
- At **100 points** flip your 100 point marker to its 100 side (it starts with the back side up).
- At **200 points** exchange it for your 200/300 point marker, 200 side up; flip that to the 300 side at 300 points.
