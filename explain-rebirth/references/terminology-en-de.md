# Terminology: German (official Frosted Games) ↔ English

The German terms are the **official** terms of the Frosted Games rulebooks (German version v1.1) – use them when answering in German. Only the German rulebooks were available. The English terms are the working terms used in this skill, **not verified against the English edition**; it may use other names. When answering in English, add the German term in brackets if the asker plays the German edition.

SCO = Scotland rulebook, IRL = Ireland rulebook, Aid = player aid.

## Place names in the rulebooks (unchanged)
Edinburgh Castle, Stirling Castle, Blair, Inverness, Eilean Donan, Kilberry, Oban, England (Scotland); Blarney Castle, Hügel von Tara (Hill of Tara), Dublin (Ireland).

## Player colours shown
Pink, Orange, Beige, Blau (blue).
