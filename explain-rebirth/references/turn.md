# Your turn (Dein Zug) – Scotland rules, also the basis for Ireland

Page conventions: see `components.md`. Everything here applies to Ireland too unless `ireland.md` says otherwise (IRL S. 2–3 refer back to these pages).

---

## Turn overview (Spielzugüberblick)
**Rulebook:** DE SCO S. 2 · IRL S. 2

Play goes clockwise. On your turn do these 3 steps in order:

1. **Place a tile** (Plättchen platzieren)
2. **Score the tile** (Plättchen werten)
3. **Draw a tile** (Plättchen nachziehen)

The game ends when all players have placed all tiles from their supplies (see `end-of-game.md`).

## 1. Place a tile
**Rulebook:** DE SCO S. 2

- Place the tile from your hand **face up** on **1 unoccupied hexagonal space** whose symbol matches the tile (leaves, lightning bolt or house).
- A space with a tile on it is **occupied**.
- You may **not** place tiles on cathedral spaces, castle spaces or water.

| Tile | Allowed spaces |
|---|---|
| Food facility | only a space with a **food symbol** or an **empty space** (no symbol) |
| Energy facility | only a space with an **energy symbol** or an **empty space** (no symbol) |
| Settlement | only a space with the **settlement symbol** |

**Exceptions (wording of the rulebook):**
- You want to place an energy facility, but there is no free space with an energy symbol "or" no free space without a symbol → you may place it on a **food symbol**.
- You want to place a food facility, but there is no free space with a food symbol "or" no free space without a symbol → you may place it on an **energy symbol**.
- *Interpretation:* the exception is meant for the case that the facility has no regular space left; the rulebook's "or" is not explained further.
- The rulebook gives no exception for settlement tiles and does not say what happens if a tile cannot be placed at all.

Image (SCO S. 2): an energy tile may go on an energy space or an empty space, a food tile on a food space; crosses mark a castle space (Eilean Donan), a cathedral space (Inverness), a settlement space and spaces of the other symbol as not allowed for facilities; the settlement tile goes on a settlement space.

## 2. Score the tile
**Rulebook:** DE SCO S. 3

After placing, check whether the tile scores and move your point marker immediately.

### Food facilities and energy facilities
**Rulebook:** DE SCO S. 3

- You get **1 point for every tile in the group** you just created or extended, **including** the tile just placed.
- A **group** = adjacent facilities of the **same type and the same colour**. A single tile is already a group (1 point).
- Food and energy facilities score the same way but **never form a group together**.
- **Example A:** Blue places an energy facility next to an energy facility of Beige. No group (different colours): Blue gets 1 point.
- **Example B:** Beige places a second energy facility next to their own: group of 2, Beige gets 2 points.
- Image: a food facility joins three others of the same colour (arrows to each tile of the group).
- *Follows from the rule:* a tile that joins two of your groups of the same type scores all tiles of the merged group.

### Settlements
**Rulebook:** DE SCO S. 3

Settlements are scored only when **completed**, i.e. when **all spaces of the framed area are occupied**. Your settlement symbols on several tiles in the same settlement are added up.

| Settlement | Spaces / frame | Points when completed |
|---|---|---|
| Small (kleine Siedlung) | 1 space, single frame | points = number of settlement symbols on the tile, immediately |
| Medium (mittlere Siedlung) | 2 spaces, double frame | most symbols **5**, second most **3** |
| Large (große Siedlung) | 3 spaces, triple frame | most symbols **8**, second most **5**, fewer **0** |

**Medium settlement**
- Tie in symbols: the player who **just completed** it gets 3, the other player 5.
- Both spaces occupied by 1 player: 5 + 3 = **8**.
- Example: Pink places a 2-symbol settlement next to Blue's 1-symbol settlement → Pink 5, Blue 3.

**Large settlement**
- Tie in symbols: the player who **just completed** it **loses all ties**. Further ties are won by the **next players in clockwise order** (from the completing player).
- All spaces occupied by 1 player: 8 + 5 = **13**.
- Example: Blue has 2 tiles with 1 symbol each; Pink places a 3-symbol tile on the last space → Pink 8, Blue 5.
- The scoring overview summarises: 1. active player loses the tie, 2. others: turn order wins (SCO S. 1).

**Harbours (Häfen)**
- When you place a settlement on a space with a harbour you immediately get **1 point** ("+1").
- If that space is the only or last space of its settlement, the settlement scoring is triggered immediately as well (example image: +1 for the harbour).

**Incomplete settlements** score at the end of the game: see `end-of-game.md`.

### Castles (Burgen)
**Rulebook:** DE SCO S. 4 · Aid back ("Burgen: Mehrheit")

You control a castle by occupying the **most spaces adjacent to the castle space**. Check each time you place a tile adjacent to a castle space:

- **A) First tile adjacent to this castle space:** place one of your castles on it.
- **B) Tiles already adjacent:** check whether you now have the **most** adjacent tiles.
  - **Yes:** return the castle standing there to its owner and place your own.
  - **Tie for most:** you win it if you have the **most adjacent energy and/or food facilities** → place your castle. **Still tied: you lose the tie** (the castle on the space stays).
- Castles give points at the end of the game (5 each, `end-of-game.md`).

Player aid flowchart (Aid back), same rule in more detail:

| Question | Result |
|---|---|
| Do you have the most tiles around the castle? | Yes → your castle: return the castle on the space (if any) and place yours. |
| Tied for most tiles, and you are **not** part of the tie | Not your castle; leave the castle on the space. |
| Tied, you are part of the tie: do you have the most facilities (energy + food, by count) around it? | Yes → your castle. No → not your castle, leave the castle standing. |
| … also tied in facilities: is your castle already there? | Yes → it stays yours. No → not your castle, leave the castle standing. |

- Settlement tiles count as 1 tile each; the **number of houses is irrelevant** (Aid back).
- **Example A (SCO S. 4):** Blue places an energy facility next to a castle space with Pink's castle; Pink has a food facility there. 1 tile each, tied (also in facilities): Pink keeps control. If Blue later places another tile there, Blue has the most and replaces Pink's castle with a blue one.
- **Example B (SCO S. 4):** Pink and Blue each have 1 adjacent tile, Blue's is an energy facility, Pink's a settlement. Blue has the majority of facilities and replaces Pink's castle.
- **Aid examples:** Pink has the most tiles around Castle Blair → Pink's castle. Pink and Blue tied in tiles, Pink has 2 facilities, Blue 1 → Pink breaks the tie. Blue places an energy facility and is now tied with Pink in tiles and in facilities → Pink, who had 2 facilities first, keeps the castle.

### Cathedrals (Kathedralen) – Scotland
**Rulebook:** DE SCO S. 4

- When you place a tile **adjacent to a cathedral space** you get **1 mission card**:
  1. You may place 1 of your cathedrals on the cathedral space. If cathedrals are already there, put yours **on top** of them (stack).
  2. Then draw the **top mission card** of the deck, look at it and put it **face down** in front of you. Other players may not see the fronts of your mission cards.
- At each cathedral space you may draw **only 1 mission card** and place **only 1 cathedral** (further tiles next to the same cathedral space give nothing more).
- With **8 different cathedral spaces** you can get at most **8 mission cards** per game.
- Fulfilled mission cards give points at the end of the game.
- **Important:** you fulfil the condition of a mission card even if you are **tied** with other players for the condition shown.
- Image: a stack of cathedrals in several colours on a cathedral space.
- Card details: `scotland-mission-cards.md`.

## 3. Draw a tile
**Rulebook:** DE SCO S. 4 · IRL S. 3

Draw 1 tile from your personal supply into your hand. Your turn ends; the next player takes their turn.

## Not covered by the rulebook
- What you do in step 3 when your supply is empty (*follows from the end-of-game rule:* you draw nothing; the game ends when all supplies have been placed).
- Whether one tile adjacent to several castle or cathedral spaces is checked for each (*interpretation:* yes, each adjacent space is checked, since the rules apply to every tile placed adjacent to such a space).
