# End of the game and final scoring (Spielende, Schlusswertung)

Page conventions: see `components.md`.

---

## End of the game
**Rulebook:** DE SCO S. 4 · IRL S. 4

The game ends as soon as all players have placed all tiles from their personal supplies on the board. The final scoring follows.

## Final scoring – Scotland
**Rulebook:** DE SCO S. 4

1. **Incomplete settlements:** every tile in a settlement that was not completed is scored: you get points equal to the number of settlement symbols on your tiles in incomplete settlements. (Example image: a 4-symbol tile in an unfinished settlement near Oban = 4 points.)
2. **Castles:** **5 points** for each castle of your colour on the board.
3. **Scotland mission cards:** score your mission cards now.
   - Note: if a card has a majority condition, you always compare yourself with the other players.
   - Note: Cathedral Bonus cards give 3 points with no condition.
   - Example image: Cathedral Bonus 3 + Most Harbours fulfilled 5 + Most Castles not fulfilled 0.
   - Card details: `scotland-mission-cards.md`.

**Note (point markers):** at 100 points flip your 100 marker to the 100 side; at 200 exchange it for the 200/300 marker on its 200 side; flip to 300 at 300 points.

### Winner and tie-break – Scotland
**Rulebook:** DE SCO S. 4 · scoring overview (SCO S. 1)

- Most points wins.
- **Tie:** the tied player who controls **Edinburgh Castle** wins (marked on the map with a white shamrock symbol).
- If none of the tied players controls Edinburgh Castle, the player who controls **Stirling Castle** (also marked with a white shamrock symbol) wins – **even if that player was not part of the tie for first place!**
- The rulebook does not say what happens if nobody controls Stirling Castle either.

## Final scoring – Ireland
**Rulebook:** DE IRL S. 4

1. **Castles and incomplete settlements:** scored as in Scotland (5 points per castle; symbols in incomplete settlements).
2. **Personal mission cards:** if you have personal mission cards (from the round tower bonus "Draw 1 mission card"), score them now. You get the **higher** point value if you completed the mission. (Example image: a card 8/5 pointing to the 8.)

Public mission cards and round tower point bonuses were already scored during the game (`ireland.md`).

### Winner and tie-break – Ireland
**Rulebook:** DE IRL S. 4 · scoring overview (SCO S. 1)

- Most points wins.
- **Tie:** the tied player who controls **Blarney Castle** wins (white shamrock symbol on the map).
- If none of the tied players controls Blarney Castle, the player who occupies the **most spaces next to the round tower on the Hill of Tara** (white shamrock symbol) wins – **even if that player was not part of the tie for first place!**
- If there is a tie at the Hill of Tara as well, the players tied there **share the victory**.
