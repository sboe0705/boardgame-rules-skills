# Ireland variant (Irlandvariante)

Page conventions: see `components.md` (IRL pages are the printed page numbers; the PDF is in reverse page order). The Ireland rulebook assumes you know the Scotland rules and only lists the differences. Setup: `setup.md`. Components: `components.md`.

---

## Gameplay (Spielverlauf)
**Rulebook:** DE IRL S. 2

- Same turn structure: 1. place a tile, 2. score the tile, 3. draw a tile; clockwise.
- The game ends when all players have placed all tiles from their supplies.

## 1. Place a tile
**Rulebook:** DE IRL S. 2 (refers to SCO S. 2)

- Place the tile from your hand face up on 1 free space, following the Scotland placement rules for food facilities, energy facilities and settlements (`turn.md`).
- Image: a food tile goes on a food space; crosses on a round tower space and a castle space – no tiles there.

## 2. Score the tile – what is the same
**Rulebook:** DE IRL S. 3 (refers to SCO S. 3–4)

Energy and food facilities, settlements (small, medium, large) and castles are scored exactly as in Scotland (`turn.md`). The following points are different.

## Wind collectors (Windsammler)
**Rulebook:** DE IRL S. 3

- They replace the Scotland harbours and are scored the same way: when you place a tile on a **"+1" space** you immediately get **1 point**.
- Image: a food facility tile is placed on a +1 space at the coast (in Scotland the harbour rule speaks of settlements).

## Extra-large settlement (besonders große Siedlung)
**Rulebook:** DE IRL S. 3

- Ireland has **1** extra-large settlement: 4 settlement spaces, quadruple frame (in the example image it is at Dublin).
- **When all 4 spaces are occupied:** most settlement symbols **12**, second most **8**, third most **5**, (fourth **0**).
- Ties are resolved as in the other settlement types (completing player loses ties; further ties go to the next players clockwise).
- If only **2 players** are in it: most symbols 12 + 5 = **17**, second most **8**.
- If **1 player** occupies all spaces: 12 + 5 + 8 = **25**.
- **Example:** Blue places the 4th tile and triggers the scoring. Blue has the most symbols → 12. Pink second → 8. Orange and Beige are tied, but Beige sits closest to Blue in clockwise order (Blue placed the last tile) → Beige 5, Orange 0.

## Ireland mission cards – public missions
**Rulebook:** DE IRL S. 3

- 8 mission cards lie face up above the board (setup). Each shows two values: **higher / lower**.
- After placing a tile, **check** whether you have completed a mission on one of the face-up cards.
- **First player to complete a mission:** immediately score the **higher** value and put one of your **cathedrals below the higher value** on the card.
- **Someone completed it before you:** immediately score the **lower** value and put your cathedral below the lower value.
- The higher value is scored only **once** per card; the lower value any number of times. Each player may score each mission card only **once**.
- **Note (connecting):** if a public mission card asks you to connect castles or round towers, you fulfil it by occupying a number of **contiguous spaces with your own tiles** that form a chain between the required buildings. Castles and round towers themselves do not count as part of the connecting spaces, since they can never be occupied by tiles.
- **Example:** Orange places a food facility and notices a completed mission. **A:** "Occupy 4 spaces adjacent to 1 round tower" – Blue already fulfilled it at another round tower and took the higher value, so Orange puts a cathedral below the lower value: 5 points. **B:** with the same tile Orange also completes "Have 2 groups of 3 or more food facilities", this time first: cathedral below the higher value, 8 points.

### Cards shown in the rulebook
**Rulebook:** DE IRL S. 1–3 (illustrations, small print)

The deck has 12 cards; the rulebook shows these 9 (it has no full list):

| Mission (as printed on the card) | Higher / lower |
|---|---|
| Have 2 groups of 3 or more food facilities | 8 / 5 |
| Have 2 groups of 3 or more energy facilities | 8 / 5 |
| Occupy 4 spaces adjacent to 1 round tower | 8 / 5 |
| Occupy 4 spaces adjacent to 1 castle | 8 / 5 |
| Occupy 3 spaces with a wind collector | 8 / 5 |
| Occupy at least 1 space in 4 medium settlements | 8 / 5 |
| Occupy 3 small settlements | 6 / 4 |
| Occupy contiguous spaces that connect 4 round towers with each other | 12 / 8 |
| Occupy contiguous spaces that connect 4 castle spaces with each other | 12 / 8 |

For any other card go by the text on the card.

## Round towers (Rundtürme)
**Rulebook:** DE IRL S. 3–4

- When you occupy a space **next to a round tower for the first time**, you may get to activate its **round tower bonus** (the marker placed there in setup).
- You get a bonus the **first time** you place a tile next to it – or, for a **set**, the first time you have placed 1 tile next to **each** marker of the set.
- You can activate each round tower bonus only **once per game**. **Several players can activate the same bonus.**

### Round tower bonuses (Rundturmboni)
**Rulebook:** DE IRL S. 4

| Bonus (count) | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Place a 2nd tile** (4×) | Draw at random 1 of the 4 tiles you set aside in setup and place it **immediately** according to the rules. |
| **Draw 3 tiles** (2×) | At the end of your turn draw **3** tiles instead of 1 from your supply (if available). On your next turn **choose 1** of the 3 for your turn and shuffle the other 2 face down back into your supply. |
| **Draw 1 mission card** (1×) | Draw the top card of the mission deck, look at it and put it face down in front of you: your **personal mission card**. Only you can score it. If you complete it (or already have), you get the **higher** value. Reveal and score it only at the **end of the game**. |
| **Doubling marker** (set of 2) | When you occupy 1 space next to **each** of these 2 markers: put your doubling marker above 1 of the 8 (public) mission cards that you have **not yet completed**. If you complete that mission later, you get **double** the points. |
| **Gain 13 points** (set of 3) | When you occupy 1 space next to **each** of these 3 markers: gain 13 points. |
| **Gain 3 points** (2×) | Gain 3 points. |

- **Important ("Place a 2nd tile"):** this ability can be activated only **once per turn**. If the 2nd tile you place in a turn is adjacent to a round tower that also grants this ability, ignore it and only score the points for placing your tile.
- The 2nd tile is scored like any placed tile (*follows from* "place it according to the rules" and the note above).

### Not covered by the rulebook
- Whether a "Place a 2nd tile" bonus ignored because of the once-per-turn limit can still be activated on a later turn.
- Whether the doubling marker doubles the higher or lower value: the text only says "double the points" (*interpretation:* double the value you score when you complete it).
- A full list of the 12 Ireland mission cards.
- Whether the public mission row is ever refilled (the rules mention no refill; the deck is only used for the "Draw 1 mission card" bonus).
- For such questions point to the publisher's Discord named in the rulebook (IRL S. 1).

## 3. Draw a tile
**Rulebook:** DE IRL S. 3

Draw 1 tile from your personal supply; your turn ends. (With the "Draw 3 tiles" bonus see above.)
