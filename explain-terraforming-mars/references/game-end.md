# Game End and Final Scoring

Page references: **EN** = English rulebook.

---

## When the game ends
**Rulebook:** EN p. 12 (also p. 3)

- The game ends when **all three global parameters** have reached their goals:

| Parameter | Start | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Oxygen | 0 % | **14 %** |
| Temperature | −30 °C | **+8 °C** |
| Ocean | 9 tiles in the stack | **all 9 tiles placed** |

- The game ends **at the end of that generation**: the action phase is played to its end and the **production phase** still takes place.
- **After the production phase**, players get **one extra chance to convert plants into greenery tiles**, **in player order**. This can trigger other effects such as placement bonuses.
- Then comes the final scoring.
- Recommendation: one player handles the scoring and everyone else checks it.

*Follows from the tile rules (p. 3, 5):* greenery tiles placed in this last step don't raise oxygen or TR, because oxygen is already at its goal. They still count as tiles and for VPs.

## Final scoring
**Rulebook:** EN p. 12

1. **TR:** your TR at game end is your basic score. Move your marker on from there as you add the other VPs.
2. **Awards:** the leader in each funded category gets **5 VPs**, the runner-up **2 VPs** (no runner-up VPs in a 2-player game). Ties are friendly: players at the same level get the same VPs for 1st or 2nd place. Details in `actions.md` (p. 11).
3. **Milestones:** each claimed milestone is worth **5 VPs**.
4. **Game board:**
   - each **greenery tile** = 1 VP,
   - each **city tile** = 1 VP for each adjacent greenery, **regardless of who owns the greenery**.
   - Count in player order to avoid confusion.
5. **Cards:**
   - first count all VPs on **cards that collect resources**,
   - then gather all your remaining cards, **including all your played events**, and count their VPs.
   - If you have **Jovian cards**, they may have to be counted separately.

- **Winner:** the highest total score.
- **Tiebreak:** the tied player with the **most M€**.

*Note on Jovian cards:* the rulebook only says they "may have to be counted separately". *Interpretation:* some cards score per Jovian tag, so you keep those tags countable before stacking the cards.

## Scoring example
**Rulebook:** EN p. 12

| Stanley's VPs | Points | Running total |
|---|---|---|
| Final terraform rating | 38 | 38 |
| Award Thermalist | 5 | 43 |
| Milestone Planner | 5 | 48 |
| 3 greenery tiles around his lone city | 3 | 51 |
| The city: 3 own greeneries plus 2 adjacent greeneries owned by Robinson | 5 | 56 |
| 3 resources on his Livestock card | 3 | 59 |
| Other face-up cards (blue and green) | 6 | 65 |
| An event card (red) | −1 | **64** |

## Suggestions
**Rulebook:** EN p. 12

- If you don't understand the symbols, read the explanation in parenthesis on the card, or see the symbol pages (p. 14–15, `symbols.md`).
- There can be many effects to keep track of, so help each other.
- **When several effects are triggered at the same time, the active player chooses the order, even for other players' triggered effects.**
