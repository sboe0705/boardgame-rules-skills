# Game Variants (Corporate Era, Solo, Draft)

Page references: **EN** = English rulebook. The standard game is described in `setup.md`.

---

## Corporate Era (extended game)
**Rulebook:** EN p. 13 (also p. 7)

- **Add all cards marked with the red and white icon** on the lower left border (a white triangle in a red circle) at the start of setup. This includes **2 new corporations**.
- Players start with **no extra production** of resources (the "1 production of each resource" from setup step 4 applies only to the standard game).
- Can be **combined with any other variant**.
- Focus: economy and technology. These projects don't contribute directly to terraforming but make the corporations stronger and add strategic choices.
- It makes the game **longer and more complex**. Not recommended for players new to Terraforming Mars.

*Follows from setup step 5 (p. 7):* with Corporate Era the corporation deck has 12 corporations (10 standard + 2 Corporate Era) to deal from. All other setup steps stay the same; the rulebook lists no further changes.

## Solo variant for Corporate Era
**Rulebook:** EN p. 13 (also p. 4)

All rules apply as usual, with these exceptions:

1. **Neutral tiles:** before you choose your cards, place **2 neutral city tiles** on the map, each with **1 adjacent neutral greenery tile**. These tiles are not yours and **don't raise the oxygen level**.
   - Reveal and discard the **top 4 cards** of the deck. Their **cost numbers** set the positions.
   - **First city:** count areas from the **top left**, left to right and then down, like reading. **Skip illegal placements** (such as ocean-reserved areas).
   - **Second city:** count **backwards from the bottom right** in the same way.
   - **The two greeneries:** count the cost numbers **clockwise around each city, starting from the top left**, skipping illegal placements.
   - **Special case:** if you play **Tharsis Republic**, you get M€ production for the 2 neutral cities even though they are placed before you reveal your corporation.
2. **Start with TR 14** instead of 20 (marked "solo" on the track) and **without the extra production** of the standard game.
3. **Awards and milestones are not used.**
4. You have a **neutral opponent** that you can steal from, or whose resources and production of any kind you can reduce.
5. You **always play 14 generations** (marked "solo").

**Winning:**
- You must **complete terraforming** (all three global parameters at their goal) **before the end of generation 14**. If you haven't, **you lose**.
- After generation 14 you may **convert plants into greenery tiles** following the normal rules, but **without raising oxygen**.
- Then you score VPs and try for as high a score as possible.

*Not stated outright:* which of the 4 revealed cards belongs to which tile. *Interpretation from the order of the text:* the first card's cost places the first city, the second card's the second city, and the third and fourth place the two greeneries. The rulebook also doesn't say how many corporations a solo player is dealt; the normal setup (2 corporations, 10 project cards to buy from) applies "as usual".

## Draft variant
**Rulebook:** EN p. 13

For more interaction. In the **research phase** players **draft** their 4 cards instead of just drawing them:

1. Each player gets **4 cards**, chooses **1** to set aside and passes the other 3 to the next player.
2. From the 3 cards received, set aside **1** and pass the other 2.
3. From the 2 cards received, set aside **1** and pass the last card.
4. Receive your last card.
5. Look at the **4 cards you set aside** and choose which to **buy (3 M€ each)** and which to discard.

- **Not used in the first generation**, because the first research phase is skipped.
- **Direction:** pass **clockwise in even-numbered generations** and **counter-clockwise in odd-numbered generations**.
- Effect: players influence which cards the others can get, and it is easier to pursue a strategy or to predict which parameter will rise first.
- Adds some game time. Not recommended for players new to Terraforming Mars.

*Wording note:* the step-by-step text says "pass the rest to the left" for the second pass. The explicit direction rule (clockwise in even, counter-clockwise in odd generations) is the one to follow; "to the left" matches the clockwise case.
