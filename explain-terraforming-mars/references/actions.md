# Actions (action phase)

Page references: **EN** = English rulebook. For the turn structure (1 or 2 actions, passing) see `generations.md`.

There are **7 different actions**. On your turn you do 1 or 2 of them in any combination, including the same action twice.

**Rulebook:** EN p. 9

---

## A) Play a card from your hand
**Rulebook:** EN p. 9–10

Three steps: 1) check the requirements, 2) pay and perform the immediate effects, 3) place the card.

### Step 1: Check the requirements
**Rulebook:** EN p. 9

- You must **meet the card's requirements** (shown next to the cost) **and be able to perform its effects**.
- **Exceptions:** you may still play a card that …
  - **raises a global parameter that has already reached its goal** (e.g. no ocean tiles left, temperature at +8 °C),
  - **adds resources you can't collect** (e.g. microbes when you have no microbe project to put them on),
  - **removes resources from any player** (red-bordered resource icons) if you can't or don't want to.
- In these cases you play the card and perform all its other effects as usual.
- **Example:** Kim can play *Asteroid* (card C) even if temperature has reached its goal, but then she gets no TR. She may remove no plants at all, or only some (maybe only Kim has plants, or the others have fewer than 3). **All plants must be taken from the same player.**
- **Cost and discounts:** the cost is in the upper left corner. Blue cards already in play may give a discount (card E: space cards). Steel and titanium help pay for building and space cards.

How the requirements of the example cards read:

| Card | Requirement | What you also need |
|---|---|---|
| A | Titanium production | – |
| B | Oxygen **9 % or lower** | Energy production, because the card makes you decrease it |
| D | Oxygen **9 % or higher** | Plant production, because the card makes you decrease it |
| E | Oxygen 5 % | Energy production |

### Step 2: Pay and perform the immediate effects
**Rulebook:** EN p. 9–10

- **First pay** the card's cost.
- Then perform the immediate effects in the **lower panel**. These effects, and other effects triggered by the card you just played, are performed **in any order you choose**.
- **Production** (brown boxes) **must be performed**.
- **Red frame** on resources or production: you may affect **any player you choose, even yourself**. Without a red frame the icon always affects **you** and must be performed.
  - Card A in effect steals a titanium production from an opponent. Cards B, D and E make you decrease your own energy or plant production to increase your M€ production.
- A **red-bordered tile** means any player's tile; a **red-bordered tag** means any player's tag.
- **Resources** are gained immediately. Red-bordered resources target the resources of **any one player** and are **optional**: card C doesn't remove your own plants even if you are the only one who has plants.
- **Non-standard resources** (not collected on the player board) are collected on specific cards, by default on the card that generates them. *Livestock* (card D) gains 1 animal (a resource cube) each time you use its action.
- **Tiles** placed by a card follow the tile rules (`components.md`). A tile **must be placed if possible**, but being unable to place it doesn't stop you from playing the card.
- **(\*) on a card:** the card is an **exception to the normal rules**. Read the explanation in parenthesis.
- If you are unsure about a card, read its explanation text.
- **VP icons** are ignored for now and counted in the final scoring. Card D may be worth several VPs once its action has added more animals.

See also the red border rules in `symbols.md` (p. 15): reducing red-bordered **production** is mandatory, unlike removing red-bordered resources.

### Step 3: Place the card
**Rulebook:** EN p. 10

| Card type | Where it goes | What still counts |
|---|---|---|
| **Event** (red, card C) | Face down in your personal event pile after being played | Its **tags apply only while it is being played** (e.g. the discount of card E when you play a space event). |
| **Automated** (green, cards A, B) | Face up in a stack with only the **top row** visible | No further effect, but **the tags still apply**. |
| **Active** (blue, cards D, E) | Face up with the **top panel** visible | Ongoing effects that may trigger at any time, or actions you can use (like corporations). |

### The example cards A–E
**Rulebook:** EN p. 9 (card pictures)

| | Card | Type | Cost | Tags | Requirement | Effects | VP |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Asteroid Mining Consortium | green | 13 | Jovian | titanium production | Decrease any (red-bordered) titanium production 1 step and increase your own 1 step | 1 |
| B | Cupola City | green | 16 | city, building | oxygen max 9 % | Place a city tile. Decrease your energy production 1 step and increase your M€ production 3 steps | – |
| C | Asteroid | red (event) | 14 | space, event | – | Raise temperature 1 step and gain 2 titanium. Remove up to 3 plants from any player (red-bordered) | – |

## B) Use a standard project
**Rulebook:** EN p. 10

The **6 standard projects** printed on the board are always available. **Each may be used several times in the same generation.**

| # | Project | Cost | You get |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sell patents** | discard any number of cards from your hand | the same number of M€ |
| 2 | **Power plant** | 11 M€ | energy production +1 step |
| 3 | **Asteroid** | 14 M€ | temperature +1 step (and TR) |
| 4 | **Aquifer** | 18 M€ | place an ocean tile (TR +1, plus any placement bonus) |
| 5 | **Greenery** | 23 M€ | place a greenery tile: oxygen +1 step (and TR), plus any placement bonus. Put your player marker on it. |
| 6 | **City** | 25 M€ | place a city tile (any placement bonus, your player marker on it) and M€ production +1 step |

- Tiles placed this way follow the normal tile rules (`components.md`, p. 5).
- **Example:** Robinson's first action is the standard project City for his 3rd city tile, again with the bonuses from Tharsis Republic.
- The rulebook doesn't say that steel or titanium may be used for standard projects. *Follows from the player board rule (p. 6):* steel and titanium are "only used to pay for cards" with the matching tag, so standard projects are paid in M€.

## C) Claim a milestone
**Rulebook:** EN p. 10–11

- If you meet a milestone's criteria, you may claim it for **8 M€** and put your player marker on it.
- Each milestone can be claimed by **only one player**.
- Only **3 of the 5** milestones can be claimed in total.
- Each claimed milestone is worth **5 VPs** at game end.

| # | Milestone | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Terraformer** | Terraform rating of at least **35** |
| 2 | **Mayor** | Own at least **3 city tiles** |
| 3 | **Gardener** | Own at least **3 greenery tiles** |
| 4 | **Builder** | At least **8 building tags** in play |
| 5 | **Planner** | At least **16 cards in your hand** when you claim it |

- **Example:** Robinson's second action, after building his 3rd city, is to claim Mayor: he pays 8 M€ and puts a marker on it. That's 5 VPs. Only 2 more milestones can be claimed now, and nobody else can claim Mayor.
- *Follows from the wording ("when you claim"):* the criteria are checked at the moment of claiming. The rulebook doesn't say you lose a milestone if you later fall below it.

## D) Fund an award
**Rulebook:** EN p. 11

- **No requirement** to fund an award.
- Cost: the **first** award funded costs **8 M€**, the **second 14 M€**, the **third 20 M€**. Put a player marker on it.
- Only **3 awards** can be funded. Each award can be funded **only once**.
- **Final scoring:** each funded award gives **5 VPs to the player who wins that category**. **It doesn't matter who funded it.** Second place gets **2 VPs**, **except in a 2-player game**, where second place gives nothing.
- **Ties are friendly:** more than one player can get the first or second place bonus. **If more than one player gets the 1st place bonus, no 2nd place is awarded.**

| # | Award | Category |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Landlord** | Owning the most tiles in play |
| 2 | **Banker** | Highest M€ production |
| 3 | **Scientist** | Most science tags in play |
| 4 | **Thermalist** | Most heat resource cubes |
| 5 | **Miner** | Most steel and titanium resource cubes |

- **Example:** Stanley pays 8 M€ to fund Thermalist, counting on his GHG Factories. At game end Kim has 12 heat, Stanley 12 and Robinson 5. Stanley and Kim each get 5 VPs for first place; Robinson gets nothing.
- *Follows from "tags in play" and the card placement rules:* tags of played events don't count for Scientist or Builder, since event tags only apply while the event is being played (p. 10).
- Banker counts M€ **production**, not M€ income. *Follows from the wording.*

## E) Use the action on a blue card
**Rulebook:** EN p. 11

- Many blue cards and corporations have **actions**, shown by a **red arrow**.
- **Each action card may be used once per generation.**
- First **pay any cost shown to the left of the arrow**; you get **what the arrow points to**. Then put a **player marker on the card** to show it has been used. The markers come off in the production phase.
- **Example:** late in the game Stanley has *Livestock* (card D). He uses its action on his turn, adds a resource cube (an animal) to the card and puts a player marker on it.
- **Effects are not actions:** blue cards may also have effects that are always active, like *Shuttles* (card E): a space card costs 2 M€ less. They have **no red arrow**, and you can always use them.

## F) Convert plants into greenery
**Rulebook:** EN p. 11

- **8 plant resources** → place a **greenery tile**, which raises oxygen 1 step (and so your TR 1 step).
- The greenery tile must be placed **next to another of your tiles if possible** and may give placement bonuses (`components.md`, p. 5).
- **Example:** Robinson removes 8 plants from his plant box, oxygen rises 1 step and so does his TR. He places the greenery tile between 2 of his city tiles and takes the placement bonus printed there, 2 plants. The greenery is also worth 1 VP itself and 1 VP for each adjacent city in the final scoring.
- If oxygen is already at 14 %, you still place the tile but get no oxygen step and no TR (p. 3, 5).

## G) Convert heat into temperature
**Rulebook:** EN p. 11

- **8 heat resources** → temperature +1 step (and so your TR +1 step).
- The rulebook doesn't say whether you may spend heat this way once temperature is at +8 °C. *Interpretation:* the general rule (p. 3) lets you play "cards and actions" that raise a maxed parameter and ignore that part, but here nothing else would happen.
