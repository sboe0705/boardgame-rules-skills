# Setup (standard game, 2–5 players)

Page references: **EN** = English rulebook. For the Corporate Era, solo and draft variants see `variants.md`.

---

## Setup steps
**Rulebook:** EN p. 7

1. **Game board:** put it in the middle of the table. Place the **9 ocean tiles** on their reserved space. Put the **temperature** and **oxygen** markers on their starting positions (−30 °C, 0 %; p. 4). Put the **generation marker on "1"** of the TR track.
2. **Resource cubes and remaining tiles:** within everyone's reach.
3. **Project deck:** make sure there are **no Corporate Era cards** in the project deck or among the corporation cards. They have a **red and white icon on the lower left edge** (a white triangle in a red circle). Shuffle the project cards and put the deck next to the board, with room for a discard pile.
4. **Players:**
   - The player who most recently won a game of Terraforming Mars gets the **first player marker**.
   - Everyone chooses a colour and takes the matching player markers and a player board.
   - **Standard game only:** you start with **1 production of each resource**, so put a player marker on "1" of all six production tracks.
   - Put one marker on **20** of the TR track.
5. **Corporation cards:**
   - Players **new** to the game each get a **Beginner Corporation** card (colourless card back) and follow its instruction: gain **42 M€** and **draw 10 project cards** as their starting hand. They can read their cards while the experienced players continue setup without them.
   - Shuffle the **10 standard corporation cards** (without the 2 Corporate Era corporations) and deal **2 to each remaining player**.
6. **Project cards:** deal **10 cards to each remaining player**. These are the cards they may **buy** for their starting hand (**3 M€ each**). Players now look at their options.
7. **Starting conditions:**
   - Each player chooses **one corporation** and which of the 10 project cards to keep.
   - Cards not kept are discarded. Discarded corporations go back in the box. **Cards are always discarded face down.**
   - **In player order**, players reveal their corporation, take its starting resources and production (if any), and then **pay 3 M€ for each project card they keep**.
   - Note from the rulebook: your resources will be quite limited for the first few generations.
8. **Start the game:** the **first generation has no player order phase and no research phase** (both were done during setup). The first player starts the action phase.

*Follows from step 5:* Beginner Corporation players don't pay for their 10 starting cards and don't choose between corporations. The rulebook says they follow the card's instruction to "draw" 10 cards to form their hand.

The rulebook does not limit how few cards you may keep. *Follows from the research phase rule (0–4 cards, `generations.md`) and the example below:* you may keep any number from 0 to 10.

## Setup example
**Rulebook:** EN p. 7

Kim, Stanley and Robinson play a standard game. Each takes a colour and a player board and marks production "1" on all six tracks. Kim is first player and deals 10 project cards and 2 corporation cards to each player. When everyone has decided, they reveal in player order:

| Player | Corporation | Starts with | Keeps | Pays | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kim | PhoboLog | 23 M€ and 10 titanium | 5 cards | 15 M€ | Puts the unused corporation back in the box and the 5 unbought cards face down on the discard pile. |
| Stanley | ThorGate | 48 M€ and 1 energy production | 10 cards | 30 M€ | Marks the energy production on his board. |
| Robinson | Tharsis Republic | 40 M€ | 4 cards | 12 M€ (28 M€ left) | As his **first action** he will place a city tile. It may give a placement bonus; his card also raises his M€ production 1 step whenever a city tile is placed on Mars and gives him 3 M€ back when he places a city himself. |

Then the first generation starts with Kim's first turn in the action phase.

## What differs in the variants (summary)
**Rulebook:** EN p. 4, 7, 13

| | Standard game | Corporate Era | Solo (for Corporate Era) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Corporate Era cards (red and white icon) | removed | added, including 2 corporations | added |
| Starting production | 1 of each resource | none | none |
| Starting TR | 20 | 20 | 14 |
| Milestones and awards | used | used | not used |

Details in `variants.md`.
