# Symbols (the rulebook's symbol glossary)

Page references: **EN** = English rulebook. This file follows the "Symbols" chapter, which also restates several rules.

---

## Victory points
**Rulebook:** EN p. 14

- **Icon:** a round picture of Mars with a number on it.
- You win by having the most VPs. VPs come from your terraform rating, tiles on the map, milestones and awards, and many cards.

## Parameters
**Rulebook:** EN p. 14

There are **4 parameters** on the board connected to terraforming: terraform rating, temperature, oxygen level and ocean coverage.

| Icon (in words) | Parameter | Meaning of the symbol |
|---|---|---|
| Orange rectangle with a globe in a laurel wreath | **Terraform rating (TR)** | How much you have contributed to terraforming. Rises each time you raise oxygen or temperature or place an ocean tile. Each step is worth **1 VP** at game end and gives income. You start at **20**. |
| Thermometer | **Temperature** | Raise temperature **1 step** (= 2 °C; range −30 °C to +8 °C) and so your TR 1 step. |
| Grey circle with two red dots (O₂ molecule) | **Oxygen** | Raise oxygen **1 step** (1 %; goal 14 %) and so your TR 1 step. |
| Blue hexagon with a water drop | **Ocean tile** | Place an ocean tile and so raise your TR 1 step, and take any placement bonus. Each tile is 1 % ocean coverage; goal 9. |

## Resources
**Rulebook:** EN p. 14

- **All resources are resource cubes** in 3 sizes: **1 (bronze), 5 (silver), 10 (gold)**. Any cube can be any resource; **where you place it** decides what it is.
- The **six standard resources** are megacredits, steel, titanium, plants, energy and heat. They are collected on the player board.
- Certain cards also collect resources (usually animal and microbe cards). A card that adds a **non-standard resource** generally puts it **on that same card**.

| Icon (in words) | Resource | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Yellow square with a € sign | **MegaCredit (M€)** | General currency for buying and playing cards, standard projects, milestones and awards. |
| Brown square with tools (hammer and wrench) | **Steel** | Pays for **building** cards at **2 M€ per steel**. |
| Dark square with a yellow star | **Titanium** | Pays for **space** cards at **3 M€ per titanium**. |
| Green square with a leaf | **Plants** | Action: 8 plants → a greenery tile on the board, oxygen (and TR) +1 step. Each greenery is 1 VP and gives 1 VP to each adjacent city. |
| Purple square with a lightning bolt | **Energy** | Used by many cities and industries, either through a blue card's action or through a decrease in energy production. Leftover energy becomes heat. |
| Red square with three wavy lines | **Heat** | Action: 8 heat → temperature (and TR) +1 step. |
| e.g. microbe and paw-print icons | **Other resources** (animals, microbes …) | Collected on their cards, which also define what they do. |

## Production
**Rulebook:** EN p. 14

- **Icon:** a resource symbol inside a **brown production box**. Example: a leaf in a brown box = raise your plant production 1 step on your player board.
- Production gives a steady income of that resource.
- **Production is not limited to 10.** Above 10, leave the marker on 10 and start a new marker.
- **Only M€ production can be negative**, and only down to **−5**. Because TR is added to it, your total income will not be negative.

## Cards
**Rulebook:** EN p. 14–15

- **Icon:** a card back showing Mars.
- Cards have **0–3 tags**.
- Cards cost **3 M€ to buy** into your hand in the research phase and have their own cost to play.
- **This symbol on a card or the board means: draw a card to your hand without paying for it.**

## Tags
**Rulebook:** EN p. 15

Tags describe a card thematically and let other cards refer to it. **There are no special rules for the different tags** beyond what is listed here.

| Tag | Icon (in words) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Building** | brown circle with a dome | Construction on Mars. **Steel** may be used to pay for the card. |
| **Space** | black circle with a yellow starburst | Space technology. **Titanium** may be used to pay for the card. |
| **Power** | purple circle with a lightning bolt | Energy production or handling. |
| **Science** | white circle with an atom | Scientific knowledge. Some cards require science tags. |
| **Jovian** | striped planet (Jupiter) | "Having to do with Jupiter": infrastructure in the outer solar system. |
| **Earth** | picture of Earth | Activities on Earth. |
| **Plant** | green circle with a leaf | Plant life or other photosynthesizing organisms. |
| **Microbe** | light green circle with a microbe | Microbes for specific purposes. |
| **Animal** | dark green circle with a paw print | Animals. Generates VPs. |
| **City** | grey circle with a skyline | Places a city tile. Often requires energy production and produces M€. |
| **Event** | yellow circle with a down arrow | A one-time event. All events are red cards that are turned face down after being played. |

## Tiles
**Rulebook:** EN p. 15

- When placing a tile you may receive the **placement bonus** printed on the area, **plus 2 M€ for each adjacent ocean**.
- There are **4 kinds of tiles**:

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ocean tile** (blue hexagon with a drop) | Place an ocean tile; TR +1 step. There are **12 ocean-reserved areas** on the board. Ocean tiles may only go there, and no other tile may. Not owned by anyone; each gives a 2 M€ placement bonus to any player later placing a tile next to it. |
| **Greenery tile** (green hexagon with a tree and a small oxygen symbol) | Place a greenery tile with an owner marker; this also raises oxygen and TR, as the oxygen symbol shows. Must be next to another tile you own if possible; with no tiles or no free area next to them, any available area. 1 VP at game end, and 1 VP for any adjacent city. |
| **City tile** (grey hexagon with a skyline) | Place a city tile with an owner marker. Not next to another city tile. 1 VP for each adjacent greenery tile, regardless of owner, at game end. |
| **Special tile** (tile symbol with a brown border) | Place the special tile described on the card, with an owner marker. Special rules and placement restrictions are on the card. |

## Red border on icons
**Rulebook:** EN p. 15 (also p. 9–10)

A **red border** means the icon targets **any player** (you or an opponent).

| Red-bordered … | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Resources** being removed | May be removed from **any one player**. May be done **partly or not at all**. |
| **Production** being reduced | **Must be performed.** If no opponent has that production, you must lower your **own** or **not play the card at all**. |
| **Tile** icon | Targets any or all players' tiles. |
| **Tag** icon | Targets all cards with that tag, whether yours or another player's. |

## Other icons shown in the rulebook
**Rulebook:** EN p. 6, 7, 11, 13

- **Red arrow** on a blue card or corporation = an **action** (cost on the left, result on the right), usable once per generation. No arrow = an always-active effect (p. 11).
- **Blue ribbon** over a box = effect/action area of a card (p. 6).
- **White triangle in a red circle** on the lower left edge of a card = **Corporate Era** card (p. 7, 13).
- **(\*)** on a card = exception to the normal rules; read the text in parenthesis (p. 10).
- **"max 8 %" style box next to the cost** = requirement; "max" means the parameter must be at that value or lower (p. 6, 9).
