# Generations (round structure)

Page references: **EN** = English rulebook.

The game is played in a series of **generations**. Each generation has **4 phases**.

---

## 1) Player Order phase
**Rulebook:** EN p. 8

- The **first player marker** shifts 1 step clockwise.
- The **generation marker** moves up 1 step.
- **Skipped in the first generation** (see `setup.md`).

## 2) Research phase
**Rulebook:** EN p. 8

- Each player **draws 4 cards** and decides which to buy for their hand.
- Each card costs **3 M€**. You may buy **0–4** cards.
- The cards you don't buy are discarded **face down** on the discard pile.
- **There is no hand limit.**
- **Skipped in the first generation** (see `setup.md`).
- **Example:** in the second generation the players have their first normal research phase. They put their hand down and draw 4 cards each. Stanley buys 3, pays 9 M€, discards the fourth and adds the 3 cards to his hand.
- With the **Draft variant** the 4 cards are drafted instead of drawn (see `variants.md`).

The rulebook doesn't say whether players decide simultaneously or in turn order in this phase, and it doesn't say what to do when the project deck runs out.

## 3) Action phase
**Rulebook:** EN p. 8 (details p. 9–11, see `actions.md`)

- On your turn you take **1 or 2 actions**, or you **pass**. Play goes **clockwise** until all players have passed.
- Actions can be combined in any way, including **2 of the same action** (p. 9).
- Available actions:

| | Action | See |
|---|---|---|
| A | Play a card from your hand | p. 9 |
| B | Use a standard project | p. 10 |
| C | Claim a milestone | p. 10 |
| D | Fund an award | p. 11 |
| E | Use the action on a blue card | p. 11 |
| F | Convert 8 plants into a greenery tile (raises oxygen), as shown on the player board | p. 11 |
| G | Convert 8 heat into a temperature increase, as shown on the player board | p. 11 |

- **Passing:** if you take **no action at all**, you are **out of the round** and may take no more actions this generation. When everyone has passed, the action phase ends.
- *Follows from the rule:* a player who has not passed keeps getting turns of 1–2 actions as play goes around, so the last player still in can take several turns in a row.
- Why 1 or 2 (p. 9): with 2 actions you can surprise the others and beat them to a goal or bonus. With 1 action you can wait out your opponents or do something obvious while you think.

**Example (second generation, p. 8):**
1. **Stanley** uses the standard project *Sell patents*: he discards 1 card to gain 1 M€, because the card doesn't help him and he wants to see what the others do.
2. **Robinson** uses the standard project *City*: pays 25 M€, places a city and raises his M€ production 1 step. As Tharsis Republic he gets another M€ production step and 3 M€ back, plus the normal placement bonus. He takes only this one action.
3. **Kim** plays the space project **Asteroid Mining** (cost 30 M€) with **8 titanium**. PhoboLog's effect makes each titanium worth 4 M€, so that is 32 M€. She could have paid 7 titanium + 2 M€, but she is low on cash and pays 8. The card raises her titanium production 2 steps; she adjusts her board and places the card in front of her. As her second action she plays another space card.
4. **Stanley** plays **Geothermal Power** (power tag, cost 11 M€). His corporation ThorGate has a discount effect for power cards, so he pays 8 M€.
5. Turns continue until everyone is out by doing no actions.

The Asteroid Mining card in the picture (p. 8): green card, cost 30, Jovian and space tags, titanium production +2, 2 VP.

*Note:* Kim overpays by 2 M€ in titanium value and gets no change. The rulebook states "no refund for overpaying" for steel (p. 6) and calls titanium "similar to steel".

## 4) Production phase
**Rulebook:** EN p. 8

All players do this phase **simultaneously**, in this order:

1. **Energy → heat:** move all cubes from the energy box to the heat box.
2. **Receive resources:**
   - **M€** equal to your **TR plus your M€ production** (which may be negative).
   - All **other resources** according to the production tracks on your player board. Put them in their boxes.
3. **Reset action cards:** remove the player markers from used action cards, so they can be used again next generation.

Then the next generation starts.

- **Example:** Kim gains 21 M€ from her TR and M€ production. She also produces 3 titanium (because of Asteroid Mining) and 1 each of the other resources.
- *Follows from the order:* energy you produce in step 2 stays as energy for the next generation's action phase; only the energy left over from this generation turns into heat.

## When the generations stop
**Rulebook:** EN p. 3, 12

When all three global parameters have reached their goal, the game ends at the end of that generation, after the production phase. See `game-end.md`.
