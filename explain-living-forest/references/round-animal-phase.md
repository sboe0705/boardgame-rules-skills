# Gameplay, Round Structure and Phase A: Animal Phase

Printed page = PDF page (German Pegasus rulebook).

---

## Gameplay (Spielablauf)
**Rulebook:** DE S. 6

- You protect the forest and its Sacred Tree from Onibi's attacks by one of three routes:
  - **plant 12 different trees**,
  - **extinguish 12 fires** (drive Onibi away for good),
  - **collect 12 sacred flowers** (awaken Sanki).
- The game runs over several **rounds** until someone has reached at least one of these goals (end of game: S. 15, see `round-end-game-end.md`).
- Each round the animals give you **elements** (sun, water, plant, spiral, sacred flower) that support your **actions**.

## A round (Ablauf einer Runde)
**Rulebook:** DE S. 6

## A: Animal phase (Tierphase)
**Rulebook:** DE S. 6

- You start the game with a team of 14 animals.
- **Everyone plays this phase at the same time.**
  - *Note:* only if you need an order (e.g. near the end of the game, to wait and see what the others do), play it one after another clockwise, starting with the player who has the Sacred Tree.
- **Reveal the top animal** of your personal draw pile and lay it in front of you. Repeat **one card at a time**, until you either **choose** to stop or are **forced** to stop (see symbols below).
- Lay the revealed animals **overlapping side by side** so that all symbols on the left edges stay visible. This is your personal **animal row** (Tier-Reihe); in the pictures it lies above/next to the forest board.
- **Empty draw pile:** at the moment you want to reveal an animal and your draw pile is empty, shuffle all animals of your personal discard pile to form a new draw pile, then reveal from it.
- *Note:* during this phase you may look through your personal discard pile at any time.
- When everyone has stopped revealing, the animal phase ends and the action phase begins.

Not covered by the rulebook: what happens if both your draw pile and your discard pile are empty; whether you must reveal at least one animal.

## The solitary and gregarious symbols (Die Symbole)
**Rulebook:** DE S. 7

- After **each** revealed animal you **may** decide freely whether to reveal another or to stop for this round.
- **But:** as soon as your animal row contains **3 solitary symbols** after revealing an animal, you must **stop immediately**.
- **Important:** one **gregarious symbol neutralises one solitary symbol** – no matter whether the solitary symbol is already in the row or is revealed later. So you can reveal more than 3 solitary animals in a round if you have revealed one or more gregarious animals. You must stop as soon as you have **3 more solitary symbols than gregarious symbols**.
- *Note:* neutralising affects only the symbol, **not the elements** of that animal – you use them normally in the action phase.
- **Fire varans** (which you may receive during the game) are solitary and bring only that symbol, no elements.
- Picture: a card with the dark solitary symbol (1 water, 1 plant, 1 flower) next to a gregarious card (3 sun, −2 spiral, cost 9); below it a personal animal row next to the draw pile, and a fire varan card with only the solitary symbol.

Consequence for the action phase (S. 8): stopping with fewer than 3 net solitary symbols gives 2 regular actions, exactly 3 gives only 1 (see `action-phase.md`).

## Magic fragments in the animal phase (Die Magie-Fragmente)
**Rulebook:** DE S. 7

You can spend magic fragments during the animal phase **as often as you like**, in exactly these two situations:

| Situation | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Immediately after you revealed a **fire varan** | 1 fragment | The fire varan is **driven away permanently**: put it back into the **supply** at once. |
| Immediately after you revealed **any other animal** | 1 fragment | **Discard** that animal: put it on your **personal discard pile** at once. |

- After using a fragment you decide as usual whether to reveal another animal or stop.
- Spent fragments go back to the supply.
- A fragment taken in the action phase can be used from the **next round** on (S. 10).
- *Follows from the rules:* the removed card no longer counts – neither its solitary/gregarious symbol nor its elements (shown by the example below, where removing the third solitary lets the player continue).

## Example: Mark's animal phase
**Rulebook:** DE S. 8

- Mark owns 2 magic fragments from earlier rounds.
- The **5th** animal in his row is a fire varan → he would have 3 solitary symbols and would have to stop. He spends 1 fragment and drives the varan away permanently (back to the supply) → still 2 solitary, he may continue.
- Next he reveals a **gregarious** animal and then another **solitary** one → 3 solitary and 1 gregarious: 3 − 1 = 2, he may continue.
- Another solitary animal follows. Mark spends his last fragment and puts this animal on his discard pile.
- He could still reveal more, but chooses to stop.
