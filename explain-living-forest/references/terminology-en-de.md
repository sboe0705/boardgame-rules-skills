# Terminology: German (official Pegasus) ↔ English

The German terms are the **official** terms of the Pegasus Spiele rulebook (2022, v1.0) – use them when answering in German. Only the German rulebook was available. The English terms are the working terms used in this skill, **not verified against an English rulebook**; the published English edition may use other names. Answer in English with these and add the German term in brackets if the asker plays the German edition.
