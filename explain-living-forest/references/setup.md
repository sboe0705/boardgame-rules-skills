# Setup (Spielvorbereitung)

Printed page = PDF page (German Pegasus rulebook).

---

## Step 1 – Personal material
**Rulebook:** DE S. 4

Each player chooses a **nature spirit** and takes the matching:

- 1 forest board
- 1 starting tree
- 3 bonus tiles
- 1 figure
- 14 starting animals

Then:

- Put the forest board in front of you and the **starting tree on its space in the centre** of the forest.
- Put the 3 bonus tiles to the **left** of your forest.
- Shuffle your 14 starting animals and put them face down to the **right** of your forest: your **personal draw pile**.

## Step 2 – Stone circle, start player, figures
**Rulebook:** DE S. 4

- Put the stone circle in the middle of the table.
- Choose the start player at random; that player takes the **Sacred Tree**.
- Look at the **green symbols** on the stone circle that match your **number of players**. One of them shows the light-coloured pawn in **first position**: the start player puts their figure on the stone next to it.
- The others follow clockwise, placing their figures on the stones whose symbol shows the light pawn in second, (third, fourth) position.

## Step 3 – Animal board and animal supply
**Rulebook:** DE S. 4

- Put the animal board next to the stone circle.
- Make one deck each of the level 1 (white sun), level 2 (yellow sun) and level 3 (red sun) animals, shuffle each and put it on its space.
- Reveal the top **4 animals** of each deck in a row to the right of the deck. These **12 animals** are the **animal supply** (Tier-Vorrat).

## Step 4 – Flame tiles
**Rulebook:** DE S. 4

- Put all flame tiles on their spaces of the animal board as a supply, sorted by value 2, 3, 4.
- Take **1 flame tile of value 2** from the supply and put it in the **middle of the stone circle**.

## Step 5 – Tree holders and tree supply
**Rulebook:** DE S. 5

- Put both tree holders next to the stone circle.
- Insert all trees in **ascending order of cost from left to right**. For costs 3, 4 and 5 make sure the element symbols match those on the holder. This is the **tree supply** (Baum-Vorrat).

| Players | Trees removed (back to the box) |
|---|---|
| 4 | none (all 39 trees) |
| 3 | **6 in total**: "1 each with costs 3, 4, 5" |
| 2 | **15 in total**: "2 each with costs 3, 4, 5 and 1 each with costs 6, 7, 8" |

- The totals only add up if "each" means each **kind** of tree: costs 3, 4 and 5 come in two kinds each (6 kinds → 6 or 12 trees), plus one of each of costs 6, 7, 8 (3) → 15. *This reading is an interpretation of the rulebook's wording; the pictures printed on the tree holders show the number of trees used per player count – use them to check.*

## Step 6 – Fire varan board
**Rulebook:** DE S. 5

- Put the fire varan board next to the stone circle.
- Put all fire varans and all magic fragments on their spaces as a supply.

## Setup picture
**Rulebook:** DE S. 4–5

The picture shows a 4-player setup: forest boards around the table with draw piles to their right and three bonus tiles to their left, stone circle in the middle with the figures and one 2-flame, animal board with three decks, three flame stacks and 3 × 4 revealed animals, both tree holders, fire varan board with varans and fragments, Sacred Tree next to the start player.

## Nobody starts with
*Follows from the setup steps (not stated as a rule):* no magic fragments, no flame tiles, no fire varans and no trees other than the starting tree.
