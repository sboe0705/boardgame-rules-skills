# Phase C: End of Round (Rundenende) and End of the Game (Spielende)

Printed page = PDF page (German Pegasus rulebook).

---

## C: End of round – check first
**Rulebook:** DE S. 13

- **First check** whether at least one spirit fulfils one or more of the three **victory conditions**. If so, **skip this phase** – the game ends immediately (see "End of the game").
- Otherwise carry out the **5 steps** together, in exactly this order:
  1. Fend off flames (Flammen abwehren)
  2. Add new flames (Neue Flammen nachlegen)
  3. Refill the animal supply (Tier-Vorrat auffüllen)
  4. Discard the animal row (Tier-Reihe ablegen)
  5. Pass the Sacred Tree (Geweihten Baum weitergeben)

## Step 1 – Fend off flames
**Rulebook:** DE S. 14

- Add up the values of all flame tiles in the middle of the stone circle (if any): the **flame value** (Flammenwert) of this round.
- Each spirit compares their **water** with the flame value:

| Your water | Result |
|---|---|
| **equal or higher** | Attack fended off, nothing happens. |
| **lower** | Onibi's attack succeeds: take as many **fire varans** from the supply as the **number of flame tiles** (not the flame value!) in the stone circle and put them on your **personal discard pile**. |

- *Note:* if there are not enough fire varans in the supply, distribute the remaining ones **one at a time in turn order** among all affected spirits, starting with the Sacred Tree holder.
- **Example:** 3 flame tiles (2, 3, 4) → flame value 9. Anna has 11 water, Kim 9, Mark 8. Nothing happens to Anna and Kim. Mark takes 3 fire varans (3 tiles) onto his discard pile.
- *Follows from the rules:* water here is your full total from animal row and forest (elements are never used up, S. 9); flames extinguished in the action phase are already gone.

## Step 2 – Add new flames
**Rulebook:** DE S. 14

The more animals were attracted from the animal supply this round, the stronger Onibi attacks:

1. First add as many **value-2** flame tiles as **level 1** animals were attracted this round (by all players together).
2. Then as many **value-3** tiles as **level 2** animals attracted.
3. Finally as many **value-4** tiles as **level 3** animals attracted.

- **Maximum 7 flame tiles** in the stone circle: once there are 7, add no more.
- If now (at the end of Onibi's attack) there is **no flame tile** in the stone circle, put **exactly 1 value-2** tile there.
- **Example:** 4 animals were attracted in total: one level 1, one level 2, two level 3 → tiles 2, 3, 4, 4 go into the stone circle.
- *Tip derived from step 3:* the empty spaces in the animal supply show how many animals of each level were attracted.

## Step 3 – Refill the animal supply
**Rulebook:** DE S. 15

For each animal attracted this round, reveal a new animal from the deck of the **same level** (if any are left).

## Step 4 – Discard the animal row
**Rulebook:** DE S. 15

Everyone puts all animals of their animal row on their personal discard pile.

## Step 5 – Pass the Sacred Tree
**Rulebook:** DE S. 15

The holder passes the Sacred Tree to the next spirit clockwise. Then the next round begins.

## End of the game (Spielende)
**Rulebook:** DE S. 15

The game ends immediately when, **at the start of a phase "C: End of round"**, at least one spirit fulfils one or more of these conditions:

| Condition | What counts |
|---|---|
| **12 different trees** | Different trees in your forest plus trees on bonus tiles you hold. Starting trees and trees on bonus tiles count as different from all other trees. |
| **12 flames** | Your flame tiles (number, not value – S. 10) plus flames on bonus tiles you hold. |
| **12 sacred flowers** | Visible flower elements in your **animal row**, in your **forest** and on bonus tiles you hold. |

- *Note:* the end is **not** triggered when a condition is met during phase A or B – only the check at the start of phase C counts.
- *Follows from the rules:* each bonus tile shows one symbol and counts as 1 towards its condition (the S. 16 example counts them this way); flowers "in your forest" include flower trees and the middle-row bonus (2 flowers) once completed; identical trees count once; flowers in the draw or discard pile do not count, only the current animal row. Because the check comes before step 4, the animal row of that round is still on the table.

### Who wins
**Rulebook:** DE S. 15

- **Only one** spirit fulfils one or more conditions → that spirit wins.
- **Several** spirits fulfil a condition → only these spirits each add up their **different trees + flames + sacred flowers**. Highest total wins. If tied, the tied spirits **share the victory**.

### Example
**Rulebook:** DE S. 16

Jule has 13 sacred flowers, Mark 12 different trees, Kim 13 flames. Anna fulfils no condition and is not compared.

| Player | Different trees | Flames | Sacred flowers | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jule | 7 | 3 | 13 | **23** |
| Mark | 12 | 0 | 4 | 16 |
| Kim | 6 | 13 | 1 | 20 |

Jule wins. (The pictures show each player's forest, animal row, flame tiles and collected bonus tiles.)
