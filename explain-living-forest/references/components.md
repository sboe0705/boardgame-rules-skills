# Object of the Game and Components (Spielmaterial)

Source: German Pegasus Spiele rulebook (v1.0, 16 pages). Printed page = PDF page. No English rulebook was available; English terms are working translations (see `terminology-en-de.md`).

---

## The game in one paragraph
**Rulebook:** DE S. 1, 6

- A tactical game for **2–4 players** aged 10+, by Aske Christiansen (S. 1).
- Each player is a **nature spirit** (Naturgeist) – Spring, Summer, Autumn or Winter – protecting the forest and its **Sacred Tree** (Geweihter Baum) from the flames of **Onibi**.
- You win by one of three routes: **plant 12 different trees**, **extinguish 12 fires** (collect 12 flame tiles), or **collect 12 sacred flowers** to awaken **Sanki**, the guardian of the forest (S. 6; details `round-end-game-end.md`).
- Each round you call on the animals of the forest; they supply **elements** used for actions.

## The five elements (icons)
**Rulebook:** DE S. 6, 9 (icons also S. 2, 10–12)

| Icon | Element (working name) | Used for | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orange/yellow **sun** | Sun | action "Attract new animals" | S. 10 |
| Blue **water drop** | Water | action "Extinguish fires"; defending against flames at round end | S. 10, 14 |
| Green **seedling/plant** | Plant | action "Plant a tree" | S. 12 |
| Grey **spiral** | Spiral | action "Move on the stone circle" | S. 11 |
| Pink **lotus flower** | Sacred flower (Heilige Blume) | **not** needed for any action; counts for a victory condition | S. 9, 15 |

The rulebook shows the elements only as icons and gives a name only to the sacred flower; "sun", "water", "plant", "spiral" are descriptive names used in this skill.

## Animal card anatomy (Die Tiere des Waldes)
**Rulebook:** DE S. 2 (cards also shown S. 7, 10)

- Animals supply elements with which you attract other animals, extinguish fires, plant trees, move on the stone circle and/or collect sacred flowers.
- **Top left: type symbol.** Three kinds of animal:
  - **Solitary** (Einzelgänger): dark round symbol with an angry face / arrows pointing apart.
  - **Gregarious** (geselliges Tier): white round symbol with a friendly face / arrows pointing together (Geselligkeitssymbol).
  - **Neutral**: no symbol.
- **Left edge, on small signs: elements** – a number next to an element icon (e.g. "2" + water drop). A card can show several elements.
- **Bottom right: cost** of the animal, a number in a sun. The sun's colour shows the level (white 1, yellow 2, red 3).
- Negative values exist: the cards pictured on S. 2 and S. 7 show "−2 spiral" next to "3 sun" (gregarious, cost 9). The rules mention a spiral total of "0 or less than 0" (S. 11). *Follows from this: a negative value reduces your total of that element.*
- Cards named in examples: flamingo-like card (neutral, 2 water, cost 3, S. 2); marmot (Murmeltier, cost 7), cockatoo (Kakadu, cost 4), hedgehog (Igel, cost 3) (S. 10).
- **Fire varans** are solitary and show only the solitary symbol – no elements (S. 7).

## Tree anatomy
**Rulebook:** DE S. 3, 12

- **Top right: cost** of the tree (number in a small green mound).
- **Bottom, on a sign: the element(s) the tree gives you** permanently, or a special effect.
- Trees readable in the rulebook images (S. 12–13); check the tiles for the rest:

| Cost | Effect shown |
|---|---|
| 3 | 1 sun · 1 water (two kinds) |
| 4 | 1 spiral · 1 sacred flower (two kinds) |
| 5 | 1 plant · 2 sun (two kinds) |
| 9 | 2 sacred flowers |
| 10 | 4 sun |
| 11 | double chevron "=" double chevron: lets you choose the same action twice (see `action-phase.md`) |

- Starting trees have no effect (S. 12). The trees of cost 6, 7 and 8 are too small to read reliably in the rulebook images.

## Forest board anatomy (Wald-Spielplan)
**Rulebook:** DE S. 3, 12–13

- Shows your personal forest: a grid of **5 columns × 3 rows** (counted from the images) with your **starting tree in the centre space**.
- **Personal draw pile** (Persönlicher Nachziehstapel) lies to the **right** of the board, **personal discard pile** (Persönlicher Ablagestapel) at the **bottom left** (labels on S. 3); bonus tiles lie to the left (S. 4).
- **Printed below the three middle columns** (left to right): 1 water, 1 sun, 1 spiral. **Printed to the right of the middle row:** 2 sacred flowers. These are the column/row bonuses (S. 12).
- **Four corner spaces** show bonus actions (S. 13): top left "attract new animals, +3 sun"; top right and bottom left "take magic fragment" (two fragment icons); bottom right "extinguish fires, +2 water".

## Stone circle (Steinkreis-Spielplan)
**Rulebook:** DE S. 3, 11

- A ring of stones around a central area where flame tiles lie. Each stone shows a **bonus action** icon in the style of the action icons from S. 10–12. Recognisable in the images: "take magic fragment" (hand over an X), "attract new animals" (chevrons + sun), "extinguish fires" (chevrons + water) and "plant a tree" (chevrons + plant); check the board for the exact order.
- Green pawn symbols next to stones (groups of 2 / 3 / 4 pawns, one pawn light-coloured) mark the **starting positions** by player count (S. 3–4).
- The rulebook does not state the number of stones.

## Other boards
**Rulebook:** DE S. 3–5

- **Animal board:** three spaces for the level 1/2/3 decks (white, yellow, red sun) and three spaces for the flame tiles of value 2, 3, 4 – in the setup picture each flame stack lies next to one deck row (2 next to level 1, 3 next to level 2, 4 next to level 3; matches S. 14).
- **Fire varan board:** supply spaces for the fire varans and the magic fragments.
- **Tree holders:** build them so that the costs and element symbols printed on the parts match (S. 3). The holders also show how many trees are used per player count (S. 5).
