# Phase B: Action Phase (Aktionsphase)

Printed page = PDF page (German Pegasus rulebook). Element names "sun", "water", "plant", "spiral" are descriptive (the rulebook uses icons only).

---

## Order and number of actions
**Rulebook:** DE S. 8

- Played **one player after another, clockwise**, starting with the player who has the **Sacred Tree**.
- First count the solitary symbols left in your animal row after subtracting those neutralised by gregarious symbols:

| Solitary symbols − gregarious symbols | Regular actions this phase |
|---|---|
| **fewer than 3** | **2 different** regular actions |
| **exactly 3** | **only 1** regular action |

- Pictures: two animal rows with arrows on the symbols – one labelled "solitary − gregarious < 3: 2 regular actions", the other (ending with a fire varan) "solitary − gregarious = 3: 1 regular action".

## The five actions and how elements count
**Rulebook:** DE S. 9

1. Take a magic fragment (Magie-Fragment nehmen)
2. Attract new animals (Neue Tiere anlocken)
3. Extinguish fires (Brände löschen)
4. Move on the stone circle (Auf dem Steinkreis bewegen)
5. Plant a tree (Einen Baum pflanzen)

- Most actions need **elements**. Your number of each element is the total shown in your **animal row** plus your **forest** (planted trees and completed column/row bonuses, see S. 12–13).
- The **sacred flower** is not needed for any action, only for a victory condition.
- You choose freely which regular action to take; with 2 regular actions you must choose **2 different** ones (exception: the cost-11 tree, see below).
- Actions are worded as "may" ("darfst … ausführen"); the rulebook does not say you must use them.
- **Bonus actions:** actions can trigger one or more bonus actions (stone circle S. 11, forest corners S. 13). You may carry them out **immediately**. A bonus action **may be the same** as one of your regular actions this round.
- **Important: elements are not used up.** If you perform the same action several times in a round, you have your **full number** of elements each time.
- When you have finished all actions and bonus actions, the next player clockwise takes their actions. When everyone has had a turn, the phase ends and the end of round begins.

### Example: Anna's elements and actions
**Rulebook:** DE S. 9

- Anna's animal row and forest board together give: **7 sun, 9 water, 1 plant, 4 spiral** (arrows in the picture mark the contributing cards, trees and board bonuses).
- She may take 2 regular actions. First: "Move on the stone circle". This triggers a bonus action "Extinguish fires", which she performs at once.
- For her second regular action she may **not** choose "Move on the stone circle" again (already her first regular action), but she **may** choose "Extinguish fires", because a bonus action may match a regular action. She does so (she could also choose any other action).
- She has 9 water for the bonus action and again all 9 water for the regular action.

## Action: Take a magic fragment
**Rulebook:** DE S. 10 · icon: hand above a cream X

- Take **1 magic fragment** from the supply and put it next to your forest.
- You can use it from the **next round** on, in an animal phase (S. 7, see `round-animal-phase.md`).
- If the supply is empty, you get none.
- Needs **no elements**.

## Action: Attract new animals
**Rulebook:** DE S. 10 · icon: double chevron + sun

- Count your **sun**. Take **one or more animals** of your choice from the animal supply whose **total cost is equal to or lower** than your sun.
- Put the new animals **face down on top of your personal draw pile**, in any order you like.
- *Note:* the animal supply is **not refilled** during this phase (only at round end, step 3).
- *Follows from the rules:* you may take animals of any level; each animal taken adds a flame at round end (2/3/4 by level, S. 14).
- **Example:** Anna has 7 sun. She takes the marmot (cost 7). She could instead have taken the cockatoo (cost 4) plus the hedgehog (cost 3).

## Action: Extinguish fires
**Rulebook:** DE S. 10 · icon: double chevron + water drop

- Count your **water**. Take **one or more flame tiles** of your choice from the middle of the stone circle whose **total value is equal to or lower** than your water.
- Put them **face down next to your forest**.
- *Note:* owning **12 flame tiles** – regardless of their values – is one way to win.
- **Example:** Anna has 9 water. She extinguishes a 4, a 3 and a 2 and takes those 3 tiles. She could have taken two 4s instead, but would then get only 2 tiles.

## Action: Move on the stone circle
**Rulebook:** DE S. 11 · icon: double chevron + spiral

- Count your **spiral**. Move your figure **clockwise** by a number of stones **equal to or lower** than that.
- Stones occupied by other spirits are **jumped over and not counted**.
- Take **1 bonus tile of your choice from each spirit you jumped over** – no matter who owned that tile at the start of the game. If you jump the same spirit twice, take 2 tiles from them. If a jumped spirit has no bonus tile left, you get nothing from them.
- If your spiral total is **0 or less**, you may not move.
- On the stone where you **end** your move you trigger the **bonus action shown there** and perform it immediately (as described on S. 9). You must have moved **at least 1 stone** to trigger it.
- **Example:** Anna has 4 spiral. She moves 4 unoccupied stones clockwise, jumping over Mark's and Kim's figures, and takes 1 bonus tile of her choice from each. She ends on a stone with the bonus action "Take a magic fragment".
- Not stated: any strengthening of stone-circle bonus actions – only the forest corners are described as "slightly stronger than usual" (S. 13), so a stone's bonus action is the normal action.

## Action: Plant a tree
**Rulebook:** DE S. 12 · icon: double chevron + plant

- Count your **plant**. Take **exactly one tree** of your choice from the tree supply whose **cost is equal to or lower** than your plant.
- Put it in your forest. It must be **horizontally or vertically adjacent** to at least one tree already there (the starting tree counts as a tree; *follows from the rule and the pictures*).
- *Note:* you may plant two or more **identical** trees. But only **12 different** trees are a way to win.
- Picture: the two tree holders with stacks sorted by cost (3, 3, 4, 4, 5, 5 on one; higher costs up to 11 on the other).

### Tree effects
**Rulebook:** DE S. 12

- Every tree in your forest except the starting tree has an **effect** that is available **immediately and permanently** after planting – you can already use it for further actions in the **same phase**.
- **Element trees:** give a fixed number of additional elements permanently (pictured: cost 10 → 4 sun; cost 4 → 1 sacred flower; cost 5 → 1 plant).
- **Cost-11 tree** (double chevron = double chevron): if you may take 2 regular actions in a round, this tree lets you choose the **same action twice** from now on.

### Bonus: complete a column or row
**Rulebook:** DE S. 12

- If you complete one of the **three middle columns** or the **middle row** of your forest, you permanently gain the elements printed there: **1 water**, **1 sun**, **1 spiral** (the three columns, left to right) or **2 sacred flowers** (middle row).
- Available immediately, also for further actions in the same phase.
- Picture: a forest with arrows from the three printed column bonuses and the row bonus.

### Bonus: plant a tree in a corner
**Rulebook:** DE S. 13

- If you plant a tree in one of the **4 corners** of your forest, you trigger the **bonus action shown there** and perform it immediately (as described on S. 9).
- The forest's bonus actions are **slightly stronger than usual**:

| Corner (as pictured) | Bonus action | Extra |
|---|---|---|
| Top left | Attract new animals | **+3 sun** for this bonus action |
| Top right | Take a magic fragment | take **2 fragments** (1 extra) |
| Bottom left | Take a magic fragment | take **2 fragments** (1 extra) |
| Bottom right | Extinguish fires | **+2 water** for this bonus action |

## Gaps (not covered by the rulebook)
- Whether a bonus action may be declined is only implied by "darfst" (may) on S. 9.
- What happens when a flame-tile stack or the tree stack of a cost runs out (for animals: the supply is simply not refilled if the deck is empty, S. 15).
- No hand limit or fragment limit is mentioned other than the supply of 20.
